How to set up Campaign Monitor in 6 steps

STEP 1 Create your account 5-10 min

Go to campaignmonitor.com and sign up. The free trial lets you test the platform, but you can only send to five contacts until you upgrade. Enter your company details and complete the account setup wizard. If you are setting up Campaign Monitor for a client as an agency, you will create a client account within your main agency account.

STEP 2 Authenticate your sending domain 15-20 min

Go to Account Settings > Verified Sending Domains and add your domain. Campaign Monitor will give you DKIM records to add to your DNS. Add these as TXT records at your domain registrar. Once verified, any from address at that domain will be cleared for sending.

STEP 3 Create a list and import contacts 15-30 min

Go to Lists & Subscribers > Create a new list. Name it and choose your unsubscribe settings. Then import your contacts: go to the list, click Import, and upload your CSV. Map the columns to Campaign Monitor fields. You can create custom fields during this step if your data has extra columns.

STEP 4 Set up a signup form 15-20 min

Go to Lists & Subscribers, select your list, and click Signup Forms. Campaign Monitor will generate a hosted form URL and embed code. Customize the form fields and design, then add the embed code to your website or link to the hosted URL.

STEP 5 Build a welcome automation 20-40 min

Go to Journeys > Create a Journey. Select 'When someone subscribes to a list' as the entry trigger. Add an email action, then a delay, then another email. Campaign Monitor's Journey builder is visual and clean. You can add conditions to create different paths based on subscriber behavior.

STEP 6 Send your first campaign 20-30 min

Go to Campaigns > Create a campaign. Pick a template from the library -- Campaign Monitor's templates are well-designed and a good starting point. Customize it with the drag-and-drop editor, write your subject line and preview text, select your list, and send a test. When ready, schedule or send immediately.

Tips before you start

  • Campaign Monitor's template library is one of the best in the industry -- worth browsing before building your own.
  • Use custom fields to store extra data about subscribers and segment your list more precisely.
  • If you are an agency, the client management features let you set separate billing and permissions per client.

Next steps

Once your account is set up, focus on building your first automation before sending any broadcast campaigns. A working welcome sequence ensures every new subscriber gets a consistent first experience. After that, review your migration guide if you are moving contacts from another platform, and use the recommendations quiz to confirm Campaign Monitor is the right long-term fit.
