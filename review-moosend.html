Moosend Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Automation

Moosend Review SMB Updated March 2026

Moosend sits in an interesting position in the market. It is priced like a beginner tool but performs like something more capable. At $9/mo, you get an automation builder that competes with tools costing two or three times as much. The tradeoff is that it is less well-known than Mailchimp or MailerLite, which means less community support and fewer third-party integrations.

If you are evaluating Moosend, you are probably coming from Mailchimp and frustrated with pricing, or you are comparing it to MailerLite and trying to decide which one gives you more for your money. This review gives you a straight answer.

Pricing: the clearest advantage

Moosend's pricing is genuinely competitive. At $9/mo you get unlimited email sends, the full automation builder, landing pages, and e-commerce integrations. There are no feature tiers within the paid plan - you get everything from the start. Pricing scales by contact count only.

At 5,000 contacts you are paying around $32/mo. At 10,000 contacts, around $64/mo. Compare that to Mailchimp where 10,000 contacts on the Essentials plan runs over $100/mo with more restricted automation features. Moosend gives you more capability for less money at almost every tier.

The only catch is the lack of a free plan. You get a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, which is generous, but if you need to grow into a tool for free before committing, MailerLite is a better fit. If you are ready to pay from day one, Moosend is hard to beat on price-to-capability ratio.

Automation: more capable than the price suggests

This is Moosend's strongest suit relative to its price. The visual automation builder supports multiple triggers, conditional splits, wait steps, and goal tracking. You can build welcome sequences, abandoned cart flows, re-engagement campaigns, and lead nurture sequences without hitting walls the way you do with Mailchimp.

It is not quite at ActiveCampaign's level - ActiveCampaign has more trigger options and more advanced branching logic - but for the vast majority of small business use cases, Moosend covers everything you need. If you have a list under 50,000 contacts and are running standard e-commerce or B2C automation, Moosend will handle it without a problem.

The trigger library includes form submissions, email opens and clicks, website visits, purchase events, tag additions, and date-based triggers. The workflow canvas is clean and fast to work with once you are past the initial learning curve.

Ease of use

Moosend takes a bit longer to get comfortable with than MailerLite. The interface is clean but not quite as polished, and the automation builder has more moving parts. Most users report feeling comfortable after a few hours of use, but it is not a tool you pick up in 30 minutes.

The drag-and-drop email editor is solid. Template selection is decent but not as extensive as Mailchimp's. Onboarding is functional but not as guided as some competitors. The help documentation is good and the support team is responsive, which compensates for the initial friction.

E-commerce

Moosend integrates with Shopify and WooCommerce and supports the standard set of e-commerce automations: abandoned cart recovery, product recommendation emails, post-purchase follow-ups, and browse abandonment. The product feed integration lets you pull live product data into emails automatically, which is a useful feature for stores with large or frequently changing catalogs.

E-commerce segmentation is solid - you can segment by purchase history, total spend, product category, and days since last purchase. For a mid-size e-commerce store, this is more than enough. For a high-volume store needing deep revenue attribution and predictive analytics, Klaviyo is the stronger tool.

Deliverability

Moosend has strong deliverability. They enforce list hygiene policies and require domain authentication on setup. Inbox placement rates are consistently good across major providers. For most senders, deliverability is not a reason to choose or avoid Moosend - it performs at the industry standard.

Support

Live chat support is available on all paid plans and response times are fast. The support team is knowledgeable about the product. There is a solid help center and a reasonable library of tutorials. Support quality is better than you might expect from a tool at this price point.

What Moosend does not do

No free plan. No built-in CRM - if you need deal pipelines alongside your email marketing, you will need a separate tool or should look at ActiveCampaign or HubSpot instead. No SMS. The integration library is smaller than Mailchimp's, which can matter if you rely on specific third-party tools. And the brand recognition is lower, which means fewer tutorials, community threads, and third-party resources compared to the bigger names.

Moosend is right for you if...

  • You want more automation capability than Mailchimp or MailerLite offer, without paying ActiveCampaign prices
  • You run an e-commerce store and need solid abandoned cart and product automation
  • You are ready to pay from day one and want the best value at the $9-$64/mo tier
  • You do not need a built-in CRM or SMS
  • Your list is growing and you want predictable, transparent pricing

Moosend is not right for you if...

  • You need a free plan to start
  • You want a built-in CRM with deal pipelines
  • You rely on a wide range of third-party integrations
  • You are a creator or blogger - Kit or MailerLite are better fits for that use case
  • You need enterprise-grade automation depth - ActiveCampaign goes further

The bottom line

Moosend is the best-value marketing automation tool in its price bracket. You get automation capability that punches above its weight, solid e-commerce features, and transparent pricing with no gotchas. The lack of a free plan and smaller integration library are real limitations, but if you are ready to commit and want the most automation for the least money, Moosend is the answer.
