Campaign Monitor Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Design Quality

Campaign Monitor Review Updated March 2026

Campaign Monitor has built its reputation on email design. The template builder is among the best in the industry, the templates themselves are polished and professionally designed, and the resulting emails look excellent across clients. For brands where how the email looks matters as much as what it says, Campaign Monitor is worth a look.

The tradeoff is price and feature depth. Campaign Monitor is not the most affordable option, its automation builder is more limited than ActiveCampaign or Moosend, and it lacks features that competitors include at lower price points. This review helps you decide whether the design quality justifies the premium.

Email design: the strongest in its class

Campaign Monitor's drag-and-drop email builder is one of the most refined in the market. The interface is clean, the rendering is precise, and templates are professionally designed with strong attention to typography and layout. If you send emails where visual presentation matters, the output quality is notably better than most alternatives.

The template library is well-curated rather than overwhelming. Unlike some platforms that offer hundreds of mediocre templates, Campaign Monitor's selection is smaller but more consistently high quality.

Automation

Campaign Monitor's automation covers standard use cases well: welcome sequences, post-purchase follow-ups, re-engagement campaigns, and date-based triggers. The builder is straightforward and easy to use. What it lacks is the depth of ActiveCampaign or Moosend - complex branching logic and behavioral triggers are limited. For standard email programs this is sufficient; for complex automation needs, look elsewhere.

Agency features

Campaign Monitor has historically been popular with agencies managing email programs for multiple clients. The platform includes features designed for this use case: separate client accounts, branded reporting, and managing multiple clients from a single login. If you are an agency running email for clients, this organizational structure is a genuine advantage.

Pricing

Campaign Monitor starts at $11/mo for up to 500 contacts. Unlimited starts at $29/mo and adds unlimited emails and time-zone sending. Pricing scales with contact count. At 5,000 contacts, Basic runs around $29/mo. This is more expensive than MailerLite or Moosend at equivalent list sizes, without offering more features in return. The value proposition rests almost entirely on email design quality.

Campaign Monitor is right for you if...

  • Email design quality is a genuine priority for your brand
  • You are an agency managing email campaigns for multiple clients
  • You want a refined builder that produces consistently beautiful output
  • You send promotional or brand-focused emails where visual presentation matters
  • Your automation needs are standard and you do not need complex branching logic

Campaign Monitor is not right for you if...

  • You are cost-sensitive - MailerLite and Moosend offer more for less
  • You need advanced automation with complex conditional logic
  • You need SMS or a built-in CRM
  • You run an e-commerce store requiring deep purchase-based automation
  • You are starting from zero and need a free plan

The bottom line

Campaign Monitor is the right tool for design-conscious brands and agencies where the quality of email presentation is a genuine differentiator. The email builder and template quality are best-in-class. The price premium is hard to justify if design is not a priority.
