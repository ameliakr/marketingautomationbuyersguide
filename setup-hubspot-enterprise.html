How to Set Up HubSpot Enterprise: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

HubSpot Enterprise Setup Guide Updated March 2026

HubSpot Marketing Hub Enterprise is the full-featured version of HubSpot for larger teams. It includes advanced features like multi-touch revenue attribution, custom event triggers, and partitioning for large organizations. Setup at the enterprise level requires significant planning.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Complete account setup with HubSpot
1-2 days

Work with your HubSpot account team to provision your account, configure user roles and permissions, and set up your business units if applicable.

Step 2
Configure your CRM data model
1-3 days

Set up your contact, company, and deal properties. Define your lifecycle stages and lead status values. This is foundational - get it right before importing data.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domains
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Email, then Sending Domains. Add and verify your domains with DKIM authentication.

Step 4
Import your contacts and companies
1-2 days

Import your contacts and companies via CSV. Map fields carefully. Use HubSpot's import templates for large datasets.

Step 5
Set up your tracking code and integrations
1-2 days

Install HubSpot's tracking code on your website. Connect your key integrations (Salesforce, Slack, etc.). Configure your ad integrations for revenue attribution.

Step 6
Build your core workflows
2-5 days

Go to Automation, then Workflows. Build your core nurture sequences, lead routing workflows, and lifecycle stage transitions. Start with the highest-priority journeys.

Step 7
Configure reporting and dashboards
1-2 days

Set up your reporting dashboards for marketing performance. Configure multi-touch attribution if relevant. Build your key reports before going live.

Tips for getting the most out of HubSpot Enterprise

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Enterprise setup is a project - assign a dedicated internal owner and work with HubSpot's implementation support
  • Get your data model right before importing - retroactively changing property structures is painful
  • Partitioning (available in Enterprise) is powerful for large teams - configure it early if you need it
  • Multi-touch revenue attribution requires proper integration with your CRM - set this up before you start running campaigns

The bottom line

HubSpot Enterprise setup is a significant project. Work with your HubSpot implementation partner or customer success manager to plan the rollout properly. The platform is comprehensive - do not try to configure everything at once.
