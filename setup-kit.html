How to Set Up Kit: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Kit Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Kit (formerly ConvertKit) is designed for creators and is intentionally simpler than general-purpose tools. Setup is fast. The free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers - the most generous free tier in the market for creators. This guide walks you through everything you need to get started.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Kit account
5 minutes

Go to kit.com and sign up for the free plan. Enter your name and email address. Kit asks about your creator type - blogger, podcaster, course creator, etc. This affects the onboarding suggestions but not your features or access.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Email, then Email Authentication. Add your sending domain and follow the instructions to add DKIM records to your DNS. Kit emails sent from an authenticated domain have better deliverability and look more professional.

Step 3
Import your existing subscribers
10-20 minutes

If you have existing subscribers, go to Subscribers and import your CSV. Kit uses tags rather than lists - you can apply tags during import to segment your subscribers. Map your custom fields if you have them.

Step 4
Create your first form
15-20 minutes

Go to Grow, then Forms, and create a new form. Choose inline, modal, slide-in, or sticky bar. Kit forms are clean and convert well. Customize the copy and connect the form to a sequence or tag. Get the embed code for your website.

Step 5
Build a welcome sequence
20-40 minutes

Go to Automate, then Sequences, and create a new sequence. A sequence is a series of timed emails sent to subscribers. Create 3-5 emails: a welcome, value-delivery emails, and an introduction to your offer or product. Set delays between each email - typically 1-3 days apart. Activate the sequence and connect it to your form.

Step 6
Set up tags and automations
15-30 minutes

Go to Automate, then Automations, and create rules that automatically tag subscribers based on their behavior - what they click, what forms they fill out, what products they buy. Tags make segmentation effortless over time. Set up a rule to tag anyone who clicks a link in your welcome sequence as "Engaged."

Step 7
Send your first broadcast
15-20 minutes

Go to Send, then Broadcasts, and compose your first email. Select your subscribers - you can send to everyone or filter by tags. Write your email in Kit's clean text-based editor. Send a preview to yourself, then send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Kit

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Kit's tag system is its most powerful feature - apply tags consistently from day one and segmentation becomes effortless
  • Write your welcome sequence before anything else - it is the highest-ROI content you will create
  • Kit's Commerce feature lets you sell digital products directly to your subscribers - worth exploring if you sell ebooks, courses, or templates
  • The free plan's 10,000 subscriber limit is generous but broadcasts are limited on free - check the current limits before you hit them
  • Kit's email editor is intentionally plain - if you want heavily designed templates, Kit is not the right tool

The bottom line

Kit is the fastest platform to get running for creators. The free plan, the clean interface, and the tag-based subscriber management make it a natural fit for bloggers, newsletter writers, and course creators. Get your form live and your welcome sequence activated on day one.
