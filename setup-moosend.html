How to Set Up Moosend: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Moosend Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Moosend is one of the faster platforms to get up and running. The interface is clean, the automation builder is visual and logical, and the setup flow is well-structured. Most users can have their first campaign live within a few hours of signing up.

This guide walks you through every step from account creation to sending your first email, including the domain authentication that most beginners skip and later regret.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Moosend account
5-10 minutes

Go to trymoo.moosend.com and start your 30-day free trial. No credit card required. Enter your name, email, and business details. You will receive a confirmation email - click the link to verify your account.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Senders, then Add New Sender. Enter the email address you want to send from. Moosend will show you SPF and DKIM records to add to your domain's DNS. Add these records in your domain registrar or DNS provider. Once propagated, your sender will show as Verified. Do not skip this step - unauthenticated emails are far more likely to land in spam.

Step 3
Create your mailing list
5-10 minutes

Go to Audience, then Mailing Lists, and create a new list. Give it a descriptive name. If you have existing contacts, import them as a CSV. Map each column to the correct field. If you are starting fresh, create an empty list and set up your signup form in the next step.

Step 4
Set up your signup form
15-30 minutes

Go to Forms and create a new form. Choose between embedded, popup, or floating bar. Customize the fields - name and email is standard. Connect it to your mailing list. Moosend gives you an embed code to paste into your website. If you use WordPress, there is a plugin. If you use Shopify, there is a native integration.

Step 5
Create your first automation
30-60 minutes

Go to Automation and create a new workflow. Start with a welcome sequence - the most important automation for any list. Set the trigger to "Subscribed to list." Add a wait step of 0 days, then an email action for your welcome email. Write the email, set the subject line, and save the workflow. Activate it. Every new subscriber will now receive your welcome email automatically.

Step 6
Send your first campaign
30-45 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a new campaign. Select your mailing list, write your subject line and preview text, and build your email using the drag-and-drop editor. Before sending, use the Send Test feature to preview it in your own inbox. Check how it renders on mobile. When ready, schedule it or send it immediately.

Tips for getting the most out of Moosend

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Set up double opt-in on your signup form - it reduces spam signups and improves list quality from day one
  • Add a sender name people will recognize, not just a company name - open rates are higher when people know who the email is from
  • Write your welcome email before anything else - it gets the most opens of any email you will send
  • Check your domain authentication is passing before sending your first campaign - go to Settings, Senders, and confirm your sender shows Verified
  • Use Moosend's preview tool to check mobile rendering before every send - over half of email opens happen on mobile

The bottom line

Moosend is one of the better tools to get started with quickly. The 30-day trial gives you full access to everything, so use it to build out your welcome automation and first few campaigns before your trial ends. Most users are fully operational within a day.
