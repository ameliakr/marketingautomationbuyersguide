How to Set Up ActiveCampaign: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

ActiveCampaign Setup Guide Updated March 2026

ActiveCampaign has more moving parts than most email marketing tools, but the setup process is well-guided and the investment pays off. This is a platform where spending an extra hour on setup saves you weeks of frustration later.

This guide walks you through account creation, domain authentication, CRM setup, and building your first automation. Budget a few hours to do it properly - particularly the automation builder, which takes more time to learn than simpler tools but is considerably more powerful.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your ActiveCampaign account
5-10 minutes

Go to activecampaign.com and start your 14-day free trial. Enter your business details. You will receive a confirmation email to verify your account. During signup, ActiveCampaign asks about your business type and goals - answer honestly, as this affects the onboarding content you receive.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Advanced, then Email Authentication. ActiveCampaign will show you DKIM records to add to your DNS. Add these in your domain registrar. Also set up domain-based message authentication by adding the provided DMARC record. Wait up to 48 hours for propagation, though it usually takes under an hour. Verify authentication is passing before sending any campaigns.

Step 3
Set up your CRM (if relevant)
15-30 minutes

If you are using ActiveCampaign as a CRM, go to Deals and set up your pipeline stages. Create custom fields for any information you want to track on contact records. Set up your lead scoring rules under Contacts, then Lead Scoring - this determines which contacts get flagged as sales-ready based on engagement behavior.

Step 4
Import your contacts
15-45 minutes

Go to Contacts and import your CSV. Map each column to the correct field. ActiveCampaign uses lists and tags - import your contacts into a list and apply any existing tags during the import. Import your unsubscribed contacts separately as suppressed contacts to maintain compliance.

Step 5
Set up your signup form
15-30 minutes

Go to Site, then Forms, and create your first form. Choose inline, floating bar, or popup. Connect it to a list and configure what tags get applied on submission. Add the embed code to your website. If you use WordPress, install the ActiveCampaign plugin. If you use Shopify, use the native integration.

Step 6
Build your first automation
45-90 minutes

Go to Automations and create a new automation. Start with a welcome series. Choose the trigger "Subscribes to list." Add an email action for your welcome email, then a wait step, then a second email. Explore the condition and action options - ActiveCampaign has far more than most tools. Use the "If/Else" condition block to branch based on whether contacts open your first email. Test the automation with your own email address before activating.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
30-45 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a Standard campaign. Select your list, write your subject line and preview text, and build your email. Use the Send Test feature before sending. ActiveCampaign's spam scoring tool shows you issues to fix before your email goes out. Schedule or send when ready.

Tips for getting the most out of ActiveCampaign

Things worth knowing before you start
  • ActiveCampaign's automation builder has a learning curve - spend time with it in your first week and it will pay off significantly
  • Set up lead scoring from the start even if you do not use it immediately - it is easier to configure before you have data than after
  • Use tags aggressively to segment your list - they are more flexible than lists and make segmentation much more powerful over time
  • The predictive sending feature (on Professional and above) optimizes send times per contact - turn it on once you have a few months of engagement data
  • ActiveCampaign's reporting is strong but takes time to understand - bookmark the Campaign Reports and Automation Reports sections

The bottom line

ActiveCampaign rewards the time you put into setup. The 14-day trial is enough to get your domain authenticated, your list imported, and your welcome automation running. Commit to learning the automation builder properly and you will have a marketing platform that grows with your business for years.
