GetResponse vs MailerLite 2026: All-in-One vs Best Value

GetResponseMailerLiteComparisonUpdated March 2026

GetResponse and MailerLite are both popular with small businesses, but they serve different needs. MailerLite is the cleanest, most affordable option for straightforward email marketing. GetResponse adds webinars, paid ads, and more advanced automation at a higher price point.

If you want to keep things simple and affordable, MailerLite is hard to beat. If you want more features under one roof and do not mind paying for them, GetResponse is worth the step up.

Pricing

MailerLite is cheaper at every tier. Its free plan covers 1,000 subscribers versus GetResponse's 500. Paid plans start at $9/mo for MailerLite versus $19/mo for GetResponse.

If budget is a primary factor, MailerLite wins. The gap is significant, especially for businesses just getting started.

Ease of use

MailerLite is one of the most beginner-friendly tools available. The interface is clean, intuitive, and well-organized. Most users can set up a campaign within an hour.

GetResponse is more feature-rich, which makes it slightly more complex to navigate. Still accessible, but there is more to learn.

Feature breadth

GetResponse includes webinars, paid ads management, and more advanced segmentation. If you want more features in one subscription, it delivers more.

MailerLite covers email, automation, landing pages, and forms cleanly. It does not try to do everything, which is part of why it stays simple and affordable.

Automation

Both platforms have visual automation builders that handle standard use cases well. GetResponse has slightly more advanced options for complex journeys. MailerLite covers welcome sequences, drip campaigns, and standard triggers well.
