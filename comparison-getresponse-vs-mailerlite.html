GetResponse vs MailerLite 2026: Comparing Two Affordable All-Rounders

GetResponseMailerLiteSMBBeginnersUpdated March 2026

GetResponse and MailerLite are both affordable, beginner-accessible tools with free plans. MailerLite is more focused and arguably easier to use. GetResponse packs in more features, including webinars, SMS, and a website builder, but that breadth can make it feel more complex.

Free plans compared

MailerLite's free plan covers 1,000 subscribers vs GetResponse's 500. MailerLite also includes automation on the free plan. GetResponse's free tier is more limited and is primarily useful for testing the interface.

Paid pricing

MailerLite is cheaper. Its plans start at $9/mo vs GetResponse's $15/mo. If budget is a primary concern, MailerLite is the better value at every tier.

Features

GetResponse does more. Webinars, SMS, a website builder, and paid ads integration are included on paid plans. If you want an all-in-one tool and plan to use those extra features, GetResponse justifies the higher price. If you just need solid email marketing and automation, MailerLite is cleaner and cheaper.

Choose GetResponse if...

  • You run webinars and want them integrated with your email list
  • You need SMS marketing alongside email
  • You want a website builder included without a separate tool
  • You are growing fast and want more features available as you scale

  • Ease of use and a clean interface are priorities
  • You do not need webinars, SMS, or a website builder
  • You want lower paid plan pricing as you grow

The bottom line

For most small businesses doing email marketing without heavy extras, MailerLite is the better value. GetResponse makes sense if you actively use webinars or SMS as part of your marketing.
