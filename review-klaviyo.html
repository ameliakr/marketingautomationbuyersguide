Klaviyo Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Automation Depth

Klaviyo Review Updated March 2026

Klaviyo is the dominant email and SMS platform for serious e-commerce brands. It was built specifically for online stores. Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and the depth of its e-commerce integration shows at every level of the product. Abandoned cart recovery, predictive analytics, revenue attribution, and behavioral segmentation based on purchase history are all core features, not add-ons.

If you run an e-commerce store and email marketing is a significant revenue driver for you, Klaviyo is the platform most likely to get you the best results. If you are not in e-commerce, it is not the right tool.

E-commerce integration: the deepest available

Klaviyo's Shopify integration pulls in your full store data: every order, every product viewed, every cart abandonment, every customer's purchase history. This data flows into your segmentation engine in real time. You can segment by what someone bought, how much they spent, how long ago they purchased, what category they buy from, and their predicted lifetime value.

This level of data depth is what separates Klaviyo from general-purpose tools like Mailchimp or Moosend. Those tools can connect to Shopify and send abandoned cart emails. Klaviyo can predict when a customer is about to churn, what they are likely to buy next, and how much they will spend over their lifetime, and use all of that to drive automated campaigns.

Automation: built for the purchase lifecycle

Klaviyo's pre-built automation flows are designed around the e-commerce customer journey: welcome series, abandoned cart, abandoned browse, post-purchase, win-back, and VIP flows. Each comes with best-practice timing and messaging built in, so you can get sophisticated automations live quickly without starting from scratch.

The automation builder itself supports complex conditional logic, A/B testing within flows, and dynamic content that changes based on subscriber data. For an e-commerce brand running serious email automation, this is the most capable tool at any price point below enterprise.

SMS

Klaviyo includes SMS marketing in the same platform as email, with the same segmentation and automation capabilities applied to both channels. You can build flows that use email and SMS together, for example, an abandoned cart flow that sends an email first, then an SMS if the email goes unopened. The integration between channels is seamless.

Reporting and analytics

Revenue attribution is Klaviyo's strongest reporting feature. You can see exactly how much revenue each campaign, flow, and message generated, with attribution windows you can customize. Predictive analytics includes predicted customer lifetime value, churn risk scores, and expected next order dates. For a brand making decisions about where to invest in email, this data is invaluable.

Pricing

Klaviyo's free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 email sends per month, enough to test the platform but not to run a real program. Paid plans start around $20/mo for 500 contacts and scale with contact count. At 10,000 contacts you are paying around $150/mo for email, and SMS is priced separately by message volume. Klaviyo is more expensive than most alternatives, which is worth it if email is a primary revenue channel, and not worth it if you are just sending basic campaigns.

Ease of use

Klaviyo has a learning curve. The interface is data-dense and the number of options can be overwhelming for new users. The pre-built flows help significantly, you do not need to build from scratch, but getting the most out of the segmentation and analytics takes time to master. Onboarding support is good and the documentation is thorough.

Klaviyo is right for you if...

  • You run a Shopify or WooCommerce store and email is a significant revenue channel
  • You want the deepest e-commerce segmentation and revenue attribution available
  • You need email and SMS in one platform with shared data and automation
  • You want predictive analytics including customer lifetime value and churn risk
  • Your list is large enough and your revenue high enough to justify the pricing

Klaviyo is not right for you if...

  • You are not in e-commerce, this tool is built for online stores
  • You are just getting started and the pricing is out of reach
  • You want something easy to set up quickly, the learning curve is real
  • You need a built-in CRM for B2B sales management
  • You have a small list and basic email needs. Moosend or MailerLite are better value

The bottom line

Klaviyo is the best email and SMS platform for e-commerce brands that are serious about using their email program as a revenue driver. The e-commerce data integration, predictive analytics, and automation depth are unmatched at its price point. The cost and complexity are real, but for a store where email drives meaningful revenue, the ROI justifies both.
