How to Set Up Brevo: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Brevo Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Brevo prices by email sends rather than contact count, which makes the setup process slightly different from other tools - you can import your full contact list immediately without worrying about pricing tiers. The free plan includes unlimited contacts and 300 emails per day. This guide walks you through getting set up and sending your first campaign.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Brevo account
5-10 minutes

Go to brevo.com and sign up for free. Enter your business details. Brevo will ask about your use case and sending volume to configure your account appropriately.

Step 2
Verify your account
5-15 minutes

Brevo verifies new accounts before allowing campaign sends. Confirm your email address and, if prompted, provide business verification details. This process is usually quick during business hours.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Senders and IPs, then Domains. Add your domain and follow the instructions to add DKIM records to your DNS. Brevo also walks you through setting up a custom Return-Path for better deliverability. Verify once DNS propagation is complete.

Step 4
Import your contacts
15-30 minutes

Go to Contacts and import your CSV. Brevo allows unlimited contacts on all plans - import your full list without worrying about hitting a limit. Create contact lists to organize your subscribers. Apply any existing tags or attributes during import.

Step 5
Set up your subscription form
15-20 minutes

Go to Forms under the Contacts menu and create a new signup form. Brevo's form builder is functional. Add the embed code to your website or use the WordPress plugin.

Step 6
Build your welcome automation
20-30 minutes

Go to Automations and create a new workflow. Set the trigger to "Contact added to a list." Add an email step for your welcome email, then additional steps as needed. Brevo's automation builder supports email, SMS, and WhatsApp steps in the same workflow - a useful capability if you want multi-channel follow-up.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns, then Email Campaigns, and create a new campaign. Select your contact list, design your email using the drag-and-drop editor, and write your subject line. The free plan allows 300 sends per day - plan your sending volume accordingly. Send a test before going live.

Tips for getting the most out of Brevo

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Brevo's per-send pricing means you pay for what you send, not what you store - import your full list from day one
  • The SMS feature is a significant advantage - if you ever want to add SMS marketing, it is already in your account
  • Transactional email is handled in the same platform as marketing - if you send order confirmations or password resets, connect Brevo via API for both
  • The 300 emails per day free limit is low for large lists - calculate your upgrade timeline before you need to scale
  • Brevo's WhatsApp campaign feature is available in some regions - worth exploring if your audience is active on WhatsApp

The bottom line

Brevo's per-send pricing model and unlimited contacts make it easy to get started without worrying about contact limits. Import your full list on day one, get domain authentication set up, and activate your welcome automation before you start driving traffic to your signup forms.
