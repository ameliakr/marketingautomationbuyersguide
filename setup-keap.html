How to Set Up Keap: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Keap Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Keap (formerly Infusionsoft) is a CRM and marketing automation platform built for service businesses. It is more complex than most tools on this list and has a steeper learning curve, but for businesses that need pipeline management alongside email automation, it is one of the most complete options available.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your account
10-15 minutes

Go to keap.com and start your free trial. Enter your business details. Keap will ask about your business type and goals during onboarding - answer accurately as this shapes your initial setup.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Email Authentication. Add the provided DKIM records to your DNS. Verify once propagated.

Step 3
Set up your pipeline
30-60 minutes

Go to CRM, then Pipeline. Create your sales pipeline stages to reflect your actual sales process. This is one of Keap's core strengths - take the time to set it up correctly.

Step 4
Configure custom fields
20-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Custom Fields. Add any fields you need to track on contact and company records beyond the defaults. Plan your fields before creating them.

Step 5
Import your contacts
20-40 minutes

Go to Contacts and import your CSV. Map each column carefully. Keap has many contact fields - take your time with the mapping to avoid data quality issues.

Step 6
Set up your signup form
15-20 minutes

Go to Marketing, then Forms. Create a form and connect it to a tag or automation. Embed the code on your website.

Step 7
Build your first automation
45-90 minutes

Go to Automation and create a new automation. Keap's automation builder is powerful but complex. Start with a welcome sequence and add complexity once you understand the interface.

Step 8
Send your first broadcast
20-30 minutes

Go to Marketing, then Broadcasts. Create a new email broadcast, select your contacts, write your email, and send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Keap

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Keap has excellent onboarding support - use it, especially in your first two weeks
  • Tags are the core of Keap's contact organization - plan your tagging strategy before you start importing contacts
  • The pipeline and CRM features are what differentiate Keap from simpler tools - invest time learning them
  • Keap's automation builder is more complex than most - start simple and build up over time
  • The mobile app is useful for managing contacts and pipeline on the go

The bottom line

Keap rewards the time you put into setup. The CRM and automation combination is powerful for service businesses, but it takes longer to get running than simpler tools. Budget a full day for initial setup and plan to continue refining over the first few weeks.
