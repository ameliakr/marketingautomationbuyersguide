How to Set Up GetResponse: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

GetResponse Setup Guide Updated March 2026

GetResponse includes more features than most tools at its price point - email, automation, landing pages, webinars, and SMS in one platform. The setup process is more involved than simpler tools, but each feature has its own guided setup flow. This guide covers the essentials to get you from signup to first send.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your GetResponse account
5-10 minutes

Go to getresponse.com and sign up for the free plan. The free plan covers up to 500 contacts. Enter your details and verify your email address.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Profile, then Email Addresses and Domains, then Add Domain. Enter your domain and add the provided DKIM and DMARC records to your DNS. Verify once propagation is complete. This is essential for deliverability - do not skip it.

Step 3
Create your contact list
5-10 minutes

GetResponse organizes contacts into lists. Go to Contacts, then Lists, and create your first list. Configure the opt-in settings - single or double opt-in. Import any existing contacts as a CSV.

Step 4
Set up your signup form or landing page
20-30 minutes

GetResponse has both a form builder and a landing page builder. For collecting email subscribers, create a signup form under Forms and Popups, or build a dedicated landing page under Landing Pages. Connect the form or page to your list. GetResponse landing pages can be published on a custom domain.

Step 5
Build your welcome automation
20-40 minutes

Go to Marketing Automation and create a new workflow. Choose the trigger "Subscribed to list." Add an email block for your welcome email and connect it. GetResponse's automation builder is visual - drag and drop blocks onto the canvas and connect them with arrows. Activate the workflow when done.

Step 6
Set up a webinar (optional)
20-30 minutes

If webinars are part of your marketing, go to Webinars and create your first one. GetResponse handles registration, reminders, and post-event follow-ups. Connect the webinar to an automation that tags attendees and sends follow-up emails based on whether they attended.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Email Marketing and create a new newsletter. Select your list, design your email, write your subject line, and send a test before going live. GetResponse shows your email's spam score before sending.

Tips for getting the most out of GetResponse

Things worth knowing before you start
  • GetResponse's landing page builder is strong - use it for lead capture rather than just relying on embedded forms on your website
  • The webinar integration is the platform's most unique feature - if you run online events, connect your webinar registration directly to your automation
  • SMS marketing is available on paid plans - if you want to add SMS later, GetResponse covers it without needing a separate tool
  • GetResponse's automation builder supports scoring - use it to identify your most engaged subscribers for targeted campaigns
  • The free plan is limited to 500 contacts - plan your upgrade timing before you approach that limit

The bottom line

GetResponse takes slightly longer to set up than MailerLite or Moosend because there is more to configure, but each feature is well-documented. Focus on domain authentication and your welcome automation first, then explore the landing page and webinar features once your email program is running.
