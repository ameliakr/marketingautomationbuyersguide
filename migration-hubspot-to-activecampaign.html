How to Migrate from HubSpot to ActiveCampaign

HubSpotActiveCampaignMigrationUpdated March 2026

Businesses migrate from HubSpot to ActiveCampaign primarily for cost reasons. HubSpot's paid plans scale aggressively in price, and many businesses find ActiveCampaign delivers comparable automation and CRM capability at a significantly lower cost. The migration involves rebuilding automation workflows and CRM pipelines, but ActiveCampaign's toolset maps well to HubSpot's core features.

This guide covers everything you need to migrate from HubSpot to ActiveCampaign without losing subscribers or breaking your automations. Estimated time: 4-8 hours.

Before you start

Keep your HubSpot account active throughout the migration. Do not cancel it until ActiveCampaign is fully set up and tested. Cancelling early means losing access to your historical data and any active automations still running.

Step-by-step migration guide

Step 1
Set up ActiveCampaign and configure your account
30-60 minutes

Create your ActiveCampaign account and complete domain authentication (DKIM, DMARC). Configure your sending domain before importing any contacts. Use the 14-day trial period to complete your migration before committing.

Step 2
Export contacts from HubSpot
30 minutes

In HubSpot, go to Contacts, select all contacts, and export as a CSV. Include all contact properties. Export your opt-out/unsubscribed contacts separately. HubSpot exports are comprehensive but may require cleanup before importing.

Step 3
Import contacts into ActiveCampaign
30-60 minutes

In ActiveCampaign, go to Contacts, Import, and upload your CSV. Map each column to the correct ActiveCampaign field. Create any custom fields you need before importing. Import unsubscribed contacts as opted-out to maintain email compliance.

Step 4
Recreate your CRM pipeline
1-2 hours

In ActiveCampaign, go to Deals and create your pipeline stages to match HubSpot. Export your HubSpot deals as a CSV and import them into ActiveCampaign. Assign deals to contacts and set deal values.

Step 5
Rebuild your automation workflows
2-4 hours

This is the most time-consuming part of the migration. Document all your active HubSpot workflows before you start. In ActiveCampaign, go to Automations and rebuild each one. ActiveCampaign's automation builder is comparable in power to HubSpot's and supports all the same core logic.

Step 6
Rebuild email templates
1-2 hours

Recreate your key email designs in ActiveCampaign's drag-and-drop editor. Test each template with a preview and a real send to yourself before activating.

Step 7
Replace forms on your website
30-60 minutes

Create signup forms in ActiveCampaign and replace HubSpot form embeds across your website. Test each form to confirm contacts are added and automations trigger correctly.

Step 8
Test and cancel HubSpot
30-60 minutes

Run end-to-end tests: submit forms, send test emails, trigger automations, create test deals. Once everything is working correctly, cancel your HubSpot subscription on your renewal date.

The bottom line

Migrating from HubSpot to ActiveCampaign is a substantial project but well within reach for most businesses. The automation and CRM capabilities map closely enough that you can replicate your HubSpot setup in ActiveCampaign. Budget a full day for the migration and a few days of parallel running to verify everything before cancelling HubSpot.
