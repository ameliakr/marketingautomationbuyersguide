How to Set Up Zoho Campaigns: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Zoho Campaigns Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Zoho Campaigns is part of the broader Zoho ecosystem. It is an excellent choice for businesses already using Zoho CRM, Zoho Books, or other Zoho products, as the native integrations are significantly tighter than what third-party connectors can offer.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your account
5-10 minutes

Go to zoho.com/campaigns and sign up. A free plan is available. If you have an existing Zoho account, sign in with those credentials to keep everything connected.

Step 2
Connect Zoho CRM (if applicable)
15-30 minutes

If you use Zoho CRM, go to Settings, then Integrations, and connect your CRM. This sync is one of Zoho Campaigns's biggest strengths - set it up early.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Sender Domains. Add your domain and follow the DKIM setup instructions. Verify once your DNS records have propagated.

Step 4
Create a mailing list
5-10 minutes

Go to Contacts and create a new mailing list. If you are syncing from Zoho CRM, your contacts will populate automatically based on your sync settings.

Step 5
Import your contacts
10-20 minutes

If not syncing from CRM, upload your CSV. Map each column to the correct Zoho Campaigns field. Review the import report before confirming.

Step 6
Set up a signup form
10-15 minutes

Go to Forms and create a new signup form. Customize the fields and design. Embed on your website or use the hosted link.

Step 7
Create a workflow
20-30 minutes

Go to Automation and create a new workflow. Set your trigger and add email and action steps. Activate once you have tested it.

Step 8
Send your first campaign
15-20 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a new email campaign. Select your list, write your email, preview and test, then send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Zoho Campaigns

Things worth knowing before you start
  • The Zoho CRM integration is the main reason to use Zoho Campaigns - set it up properly and it pays dividends
  • Zoho Campaigns has a generous free plan - it covers most small business needs without upgrading
  • Use topic-based subscriptions if you send different types of content to the same audience - it reduces unsubscribes
  • The predesigned templates are decent but customization is limited compared to tools like Campaign Monitor

The bottom line

Zoho Campaigns is best used as part of the Zoho ecosystem. If you are already on Zoho CRM, the integration makes it a natural choice. Standalone, it is a capable but not exceptional tool. Get your domain authenticated and your CRM sync working first.
