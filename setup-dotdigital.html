How to Set Up Dotdigital: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Dotdigital Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Dotdigital is a mid-market omnichannel marketing platform covering email, SMS, push notifications, and more. Setup is more involved than simpler tools - plan for a full day to get the core features working properly.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Set up your account
15-30 minutes

Dotdigital accounts are typically provisioned by the sales team. Complete your account setup including sender details and company information.

Step 2
Configure sending domain and IP warming
1-2 days

Go to Settings, then Sender Authentication. Add DKIM and SPF records to your DNS. If you are on a dedicated IP, follow Dotdigital's IP warming schedule before sending large volumes.

Step 3
Import your contacts
20-40 minutes

Go to Contacts and import your CSV. Map fields carefully. Dotdigital has extensive contact data fields - use them to enable rich segmentation later.

Step 4
Set up address books
10-15 minutes

Organize your contacts into address books. Plan your address book structure around how you want to segment and target.

Step 5
Configure your signup forms
20-30 minutes

Go to Surveys and Forms. Build your signup forms and connect them to the appropriate address books. Embed on your website.

Step 6
Build your welcome program
30-60 minutes

Go to Automation and create a new program. Set up your welcome journey with email and optional SMS steps. Test before activating.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a new email campaign. Use the drag-and-drop builder, select your address book, preview and test, then send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Dotdigital

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Work closely with your Dotdigital account manager during setup - they can significantly speed up the process
  • IP warming is critical if you are on a dedicated IP - do not skip it or your deliverability will suffer
  • Dotdigital's segmentation and dynamic content capabilities are advanced - invest time learning them
  • The SMS channel is tightly integrated - consider using it alongside email for key journeys

The bottom line

Dotdigital is a serious platform that rewards proper setup. Work with your account manager during onboarding and do not skip the deliverability configuration. The multi-channel capabilities are its key differentiator.
