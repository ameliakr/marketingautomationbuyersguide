How to Migrate from Mailchimp to Moosend

Mailchimp Moosend Migration Updated March 2026

Moosend is one of the most popular destinations for people leaving Mailchimp. It starts at the same price point, has a stronger automation builder, and doesn't impose the kind of contact-count pricing that catches Mailchimp users off guard as their lists grow.

The migration itself is one of the more manageable platform switches you can make. Moosend imports contacts cleanly from a CSV, the interface is intuitive, and you can have your first campaign live within a few hours of signing up.

This guide walks you through every step, including what to export before you cancel Mailchimp and what to watch out for during the import.

Step-by-step migration guide

Step 1
Export your contacts from Mailchimp
15-30 minutes

Go to Audience in Mailchimp and export your full audience as a CSV. Make sure you include all merge fields (custom fields) and tags. Export your unsubscribe and bounce lists separately - you will need to suppress these in Moosend to stay compliant and protect your deliverability.

Step 2
Document your automations
15-60 minutes

Go through every active Customer Journey or automation in Mailchimp and write down or screenshot each one. Note the trigger, any conditions or splits, the email content, and the timing delays. You will not be able to access Mailchimp after cancellation, so do this before anything else.

Step 3
Export your email templates
30-60 minutes

Save the HTML of any email templates you want to reuse. In Mailchimp, open each template and export or copy the HTML. Moosend's template editor supports HTML import, or you can rebuild templates using their drag-and-drop editor which is clean and fast to work with.

Step 4
Download your campaign reports
10-15 minutes

Export your historical campaign data from Mailchimp Reports. Open rates, click rates, and subscriber growth data will not transfer to Moosend and you will lose access once your Mailchimp account is closed.

Step 5
Set up your Moosend account
30-45 minutes

Complete your Moosend account setup. Authenticate your sending domain by adding the SPF and DKIM records to your domain's DNS settings. This is the most important step for deliverability - emails from an unauthenticated domain are far more likely to land in spam. Moosend's setup guide walks you through the DNS changes step by step.

Step 6
Import your contacts
20-45 minutes

In Moosend, go to Audience and create a new mailing list, then import your CSV. Map each column to the correct field. If you have tags in Mailchimp, decide whether to map them to Moosend custom fields or recreate them as segments. Import your suppression list (unsubscribes and bounces) separately to prevent sending to contacts who have opted out.

Step 7
Rebuild your automations
1-3 hours

Use Moosend's automation builder to recreate your workflows. Moosend's trigger and action options are more extensive than Mailchimp's. Start with your welcome series and any high-priority nurture sequences. Test each automation with a test subscriber before activating it.

Step 8
Update your signup forms
30-60 minutes

Replace your Mailchimp forms with Moosend forms on your website. Moosend has an embeddable form builder and integrates with most website platforms. Update any popups, landing page tools, or third-party integrations that were pointed at Mailchimp.

Step 9
Test before going live
1-2 hours

Send test campaigns to yourself. Trigger your automations manually using a test contact and verify each sequence fires correctly. Check all links, verify unsubscribe functionality works, and confirm your domain authentication is passing. Do not cancel Mailchimp until this is done.

Step 10
Cancel Mailchimp
5 minutes

Once everything is working in Moosend, cancel your Mailchimp account. Check your billing date first to avoid being charged for an extra month. In Mailchimp, go to Account then Settings then Pause or Delete Account.

Before you cancel Mailchimp

Export your full contact list, suppression list, campaign history, and any templates you want to keep. Update all connected integrations (Shopify, WordPress, Zapier, etc.) to point to Moosend. Once your Mailchimp account is closed, none of this data is recoverable.

How long does the migration take?

Most businesses can complete the full migration in two to four hours. The contact import is fast - under an hour for most list sizes. The time goes into rebuilding automations and updating your forms and integrations. If you are only running a simple welcome sequence, you can realistically be fully live in Moosend within an afternoon.

Why people switch from Mailchimp to Moosend

The most common reasons are pricing and automation depth. Mailchimp's pricing scales quickly as your list grows and the free plan is limited to 500 contacts. Moosend starts at $9/mo and gives you more automation capability at every tier. If you have been bumping against Mailchimp's automation limitations - wanting more complex branching logic, more trigger options, or better e-commerce integration - Moosend is a meaningful upgrade.
