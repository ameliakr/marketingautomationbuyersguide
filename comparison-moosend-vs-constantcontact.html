Moosend vs Constant Contact 2026: Automation Value vs Simple and Supported

MoosendConstant ContactComparisonUpdated March 2026

Moosend and Constant Contact are both aimed at small businesses, but they have taken different paths. Moosend offers strong automation capability at a low price with a modern interface. Constant Contact has been around for over two decades and trades on simplicity, event management, and a reputation for solid customer support.

For most businesses comparing these two today, Moosend offers more capability for less money. Constant Contact is the better fit if you run events or specifically value its long customer support track record.

Automation

Moosend has the stronger automation builder. The visual workflow editor supports advanced conditional logic, e-commerce triggers, and multi-step behavioral sequences. It punches above its price point on automation capability.

Constant Contact's automation covers the basics. Welcome emails, anniversary sequences, simple post-signup flows. It is adequate for businesses with straightforward needs.

Events

Constant Contact's standout differentiator is built-in event management. You can create event registration pages, sell tickets, send invitations, and automate follow-up emails based on attendance. For nonprofits, associations, and local businesses with regular events, this is a genuine reason to choose Constant Contact.

Moosend has no event management features.

Pricing

Moosend starts at $9/mo and delivers strong value at that price. Constant Contact starts at $12/mo and scales quickly with list size. Neither has a free plan, though both offer extended trials.

For comparable automation capability, Moosend is the better value.
