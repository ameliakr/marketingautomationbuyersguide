Brevo vs ActiveCampaign 2026: Budget Friendly vs Deep Automation

BrevoActiveCampaignComparisonUpdated March 2026

Brevo and ActiveCampaign both offer email, automation, and SMS, but they sit at different points on the power-versus-simplicity spectrum. Brevo is more affordable, easier to learn, and strong enough for most small businesses. ActiveCampaign goes deeper on automation complexity and CRM capability.

If your marketing program is standard and budget matters, Brevo delivers strong value. If you need advanced multi-step automation and a real sales pipeline, ActiveCampaign is worth the extra cost.

Pricing

Brevo has a meaningful price advantage. Its free plan includes unlimited contacts with up to 300 emails per day. Paid plans start at $9/mo based on send volume. ActiveCampaign starts at $15/mo with no free plan.

For businesses with large lists that they do not email frequently, Brevo can be significantly cheaper. For businesses that email smaller engaged lists regularly, the difference narrows.

Automation

ActiveCampaign has the more powerful automation builder. It supports 75+ triggers, unlimited actions per workflow, goal-based automation, and automations that trigger other automations. If your business runs complex multi-path customer journeys, ActiveCampaign handles them better.

Brevo's automation builder handles standard workflows well: welcome sequences, drip campaigns, behavior-triggered emails, and multi-channel flows including SMS. For most small businesses this is sufficient.

CRM

ActiveCampaign includes a real CRM with deal pipelines, contact and lead scoring, and sales automation. Marketing and sales data share the same contact record. Brevo has basic CRM functionality but no true pipeline or scoring system.

If you manage a sales team or need to track deals alongside marketing, ActiveCampaign is the stronger choice.

Transactional email

Brevo supports transactional email in the same platform, making it useful for e-commerce and SaaS businesses that send order confirmations, password resets, and other automated triggered emails. ActiveCampaign does not.

Brevo is the better choice for budget-conscious businesses with standard automation needs, especially if you have a large list you do not email frequently. ActiveCampaign is worth the premium for businesses that need deep automation and a real CRM.
