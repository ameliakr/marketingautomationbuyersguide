Mailchimp vs AWeber 2026: The New Standard vs The Original

MailchimpAWeberSMBEmail marketingUpdated March 2026

AWeber was one of the first email marketing tools. Mailchimp came along and gradually became the default choice for small businesses. Both are solid, beginner-friendly tools, but Mailchimp has clearly won more market share. The question is whether AWeber still has advantages worth considering.

Pricing

AWeber's free plan covers up to 500 subscribers with 3,000 emails/mo. Mailchimp's free plan is 500 contacts and 1,000 emails/mo. AWeber's free plan is slightly more generous on sends. Paid plans are similarly priced.

Ease of use

Both are designed for beginners and are easy to navigate. Mailchimp's interface is more modern. AWeber's interface has improved but still feels slightly dated compared to Mailchimp. Either will work for non-technical users.

Automation

Both have basic automation. AWeber's automation (called Campaigns) covers standard sequences and triggers. Mailchimp's automation is comparable at the same price tiers. Neither is a power automation tool: for that you need ActiveCampaign or Brevo.

Support

AWeber offers 24/7 live chat and email support on all plans including free. Mailchimp's support is more limited, particularly on the free plan. For users who want accessible support, AWeber has an advantage.

Choose Mailchimp if...

  • You want the most widely supported integrations
  • You prefer a more modern interface and design tools
  • You want better analytics at the same price point

The bottom line

Mailchimp is the more modern choice with better integrations and a larger community. AWeber's main differentiator is its 24/7 support, which matters more than people expect when something goes wrong before an important send. For most new users, Mailchimp is the natural starting point. AWeber is worth considering if support is a priority.
