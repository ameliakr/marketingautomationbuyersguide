How to Set Up HubSpot: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

HubSpot Setup Guide Updated March 2026

HubSpot's free plan is the most generous CRM offer in the market. Setting it up properly takes a bit more time than simpler tools because there is more to configure - but the payoff is a platform that manages your contacts, deals, and marketing in one place.

This guide covers the free plan setup. If you are on a paid Marketing Hub tier, the same steps apply but with access to additional features at each stage.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your HubSpot account
5-10 minutes

Go to hubspot.com and sign up for free. Choose the free CRM option. Enter your business details. HubSpot will ask about your company size and what you want to use the platform for - this affects the onboarding content but not your access.

Step 2
Complete your account setup
15-20 minutes

Go through HubSpot's setup checklist in the dashboard. Add your company logo, set your time zone, and configure your notification preferences. Connect your email (Gmail or Outlook) to log emails and track opens directly from your inbox using HubSpot's browser extension.

Step 3
Set up your email sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Marketing, then Email, then Sending Domains. Add your sending domain and follow the instructions to add DKIM records to your DNS. This is required before you can send marketing emails from your own domain rather than via HubSpot's shared domain.

Step 4
Import your contacts
15-30 minutes

Go to Contacts and import your CSV. Map each column to HubSpot properties - note that HubSpot uses a different data model than most email tools. Contacts are associated with Companies and Deals as separate objects. During import you can create company records automatically based on email domain.

Step 5
Set up your forms
15-20 minutes

Go to Marketing, then Forms, and create your first form. HubSpot forms automatically create contacts in your CRM when submitted. Embed the form on your website using the provided code, or use the HubSpot WordPress plugin or Shopify integration.

Step 6
Create a contact list
5-10 minutes

Go to Contacts, then Lists, and create a new list. HubSpot lists are dynamic - they update automatically based on criteria you define. Create an Active List for all marketing contacts. You will use this list to send your first campaign.

Step 7
Send your first email campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Marketing, then Email, and create a new email. On the free plan you are limited to 2,000 sends per month. Choose a template, write your subject line and content, select your list, and send a test to yourself. Check it on mobile before sending. Schedule or send immediately.

Step 8
Set up a basic automation
20-40 minutes

On the free plan, automation is limited to simple follow-up sequences. Go to Automation, then Sequences (if you have Sales Hub access) or Marketing, then Workflows on paid plans. On the free Marketing Hub, create a simple welcome email using the Email Automation feature under each form.

Tips for getting the most out of HubSpot

Things worth knowing before you start
  • HubSpot's data model takes time to understand - contacts, companies, and deals are separate objects that associate with each other
  • The free plan is genuinely useful but has a 2,000 email send limit per month - plan your sending accordingly
  • Install the HubSpot Chrome extension to log emails and get open tracking directly in Gmail or Outlook
  • Use HubSpot's meeting booking link (available free) and share it in emails to eliminate back-and-forth scheduling
  • The CRM is the main reason to choose HubSpot - make sure you are using contact and deal records, not just the email tool

The bottom line

HubSpot's free plan gives you a fully functional CRM and basic email marketing at no cost. The setup takes a few hours done properly. Focus on getting your contacts imported and your domain authenticated first - everything else builds from there.
