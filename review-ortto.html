Ortto Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Built-In CDP

Ortto Review Updated March 2026

Ortto (formerly Autopilot) is a marketing automation platform built around a customer data platform. Where most tools store basic contact information and email engagement data, Ortto aggregates data from across your tech stack - CRM, product analytics, support, billing - into unified customer profiles that drive more sophisticated segmentation and automation.

Ortto sits in an interesting middle ground: more capable than SMB tools like ActiveCampaign on the data side, but more accessible than enterprise platforms like Marketo. It is a legitimate option for data-conscious B2B SaaS companies that want marketing automation and a CDP without the enterprise price tag.

The CDP advantage

Ortto's built-in CDP is what differentiates it from tools like ActiveCampaign or HubSpot. You can pull in data from Salesforce, HubSpot, Segment, Stripe, Intercom, and other tools to build a complete picture of each customer. Segmentation based on product usage, billing status, support history, and marketing engagement is possible without a separate CDP or complex data engineering.

Automation and journeys

Ortto's visual automation builder supports multi-step journeys with conditional branching based on any data in the CDP. For a SaaS company wanting to trigger onboarding sequences based on feature adoption, send win-back campaigns to churned customers, or personalize campaigns based on plan type, Ortto handles these use cases well.

Pricing and target market

Ortto starts at $99/mo, which puts it above SMB tools but well below enterprise platforms. The sweet spot is a B2B SaaS company with 2,000-20,000 contacts that wants to move beyond ActiveCampaign's capabilities without moving to a full enterprise platform. The data integration capabilities justify the price premium for teams that will actually use them.

Ortto is right for you if...

  • You are a B2B SaaS company that wants marketing automation with unified customer data
  • You need to trigger automations based on product usage, billing events, or support data
  • You have outgrown ActiveCampaign but are not ready for a full enterprise platform
  • Your team has the technical capability to set up data integrations

Ortto is not right for you if...

  • You are a small business or early-stage startup - pricing is not appropriate
  • You primarily need email marketing without complex data integration
  • You run an e-commerce store - Klaviyo is a better fit
  • You want quick self-serve setup with minimal technical overhead

The bottom line

Ortto is the right platform for B2B SaaS companies that want the combination of marketing automation and a customer data platform without the complexity and cost of an enterprise solution.
