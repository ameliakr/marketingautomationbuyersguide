How to Set Up Klaviyo: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Klaviyo Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Klaviyo has the deepest e-commerce integration available and the most capable automation flows for online stores. The setup takes longer than simpler tools, but once complete you have a powerful revenue-generating engine running automatically. This guide covers the full setup for a Shopify or WooCommerce store.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Klaviyo account
5-10 minutes

Go to klaviyo.com and sign up. The free plan covers up to 250 contacts. Enter your business details and proceed to connect your store.

Step 2
Connect your e-commerce store
15-30 minutes

Go to Integrations and connect Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, or your platform. The Shopify integration is the most seamless - install the Klaviyo app from the Shopify App Store and authorize the connection. Klaviyo will sync your full order history, customer data, and product catalog. This sync can take an hour or more for stores with significant history.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Settings, then Email Address Sending Domains. Add your domain and follow the DKIM and SPF authentication instructions. Klaviyo provides a dedicated sending domain for your account - configure this to improve deliverability for high-volume senders.

Step 4
Set up your core flows
60-120 minutes

Go to Flows and use Klaviyo's flow library to activate your core automations. Start with these in order: Welcome Series (triggers when someone joins your email list), Abandoned Cart (triggers when someone adds to cart but does not purchase), Browse Abandonment (triggers when someone views a product but does not add to cart), and Post-Purchase (triggers after a customer completes an order). Customize the email content in each flow and set appropriate timing delays.

Step 5
Configure your signup form
20-30 minutes

Go to Sign-Up Forms and create a popup for your store. Klaviyo forms can collect email and SMS opt-in simultaneously. Set the form to appear after a delay or on exit intent. Connect it to a list that triggers your Welcome Series flow. Customize the design to match your brand.

Step 6
Build your first segment
15-20 minutes

Go to Lists and Segments and create a new segment. Start with "Engaged subscribers" - contacts who have opened or clicked in the last 90 days. Use this segment for your regular campaigns to protect your deliverability by not sending to unengaged contacts.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create an Email campaign. Select your engaged subscriber segment, design your email, add a product block to pull product recommendations, and write your subject line. Send a preview before going live.

Tips for getting the most out of Klaviyo

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Activate abandoned cart first - it is typically the highest-revenue flow and the fastest to recover from your setup investment
  • Klaviyo's predictive analytics (predicted CLV, churn risk, next order date) become available after your store has been connected for a few weeks
  • Use Klaviyo's benchmarks feature to compare your email performance against industry averages in your niche
  • Browse abandonment flows require Klaviyo's tracking snippet on your website - install it early to start collecting data
  • Segment your list by engagement level from day one - sending to unengaged contacts damages deliverability over time

The bottom line

Klaviyo's setup takes more time than simpler tools but the automation flows pay for themselves quickly. Prioritize connecting your store, activating abandoned cart, and getting your signup form live. The rest can be refined over time as your data builds.
