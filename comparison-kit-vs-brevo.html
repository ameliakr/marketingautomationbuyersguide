Creator vs business focus

Kit is built around creators: people building audiences around content. Brevo is built for businesses: companies sending marketing, transactional, and SMS messages to customers. The tools reflect these different priorities.

Free plan

Kit's free plan is remarkably generous: up to 10,000 subscribers with broadcasts and automations included. Brevo's free plan offers unlimited contacts with 9,000 sends/mo. Both are excellent free tiers; Kit's is better for audience building, Brevo's is better for high-frequency sending to large lists.

Multi-channel

Brevo includes SMS, WhatsApp, and transactional email. Kit is email-first with no SMS. For businesses that need multiple channels, Brevo wins. For creators who just need email and landing pages, Kit is sufficient.

Commerce

Kit includes tools for selling digital products: courses, ebooks, and subscriptions. Brevo does not. For creators who monetize through digital products, Kit's commerce tools add real value.

Choose Kit if...

  • You are a creator building an audience around content
  • You want to sell digital products through your email platform
  • You want a very generous free plan for a large subscriber list
  • Newsletter-style sending is your primary use case
