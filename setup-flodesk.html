How to Set Up Flodesk: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Flodesk Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Flodesk is designed for creators and small businesses who want beautiful emails without a design background. The flat-rate pricing model means your cost does not increase as your list grows, which is a significant advantage for growing businesses.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your account
5-10 minutes

Go to flodesk.com and sign up for a 30-day free trial. No credit card required. Enter your name and business details.

Step 2
Set up your brand
10-15 minutes

Go to Brand Settings and add your logo, brand colors, and fonts. Flodesk uses these across all your emails and forms automatically, which saves significant time later.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Account Settings, then Sending Domain. Add your domain and follow the instructions to add DKIM records. Verify once propagated. This removes Flodesk branding from your emails and improves deliverability.

Step 4
Import your subscribers
10-15 minutes

Go to Audience and import your CSV. Map your columns and assign subscribers to a segment. Flodesk uses segments rather than lists.

Step 5
Create a signup form
15-20 minutes

Go to Forms and create a new form. Flodesk's form builder is visual and produces beautiful results. Connect it to a segment and embed or share as a standalone page.

Step 6
Build a welcome workflow
20-30 minutes

Go to Workflows and create a new workflow. Set the trigger to a form submission or segment join. Add an email step, design your welcome email using Flodesk's templates, and activate.

Step 7
Send your first email
15-20 minutes

Go to Emails and create a new email. Choose a template, customize it, select your segment, and send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Flodesk

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Spend time on your brand settings first - Flodesk auto-applies your branding everywhere, so getting it right upfront saves time
  • Flodesk's flat-rate pricing means there is no financial penalty for list growth - import all your contacts without worrying about cost
  • The checkout feature (paid plan) lets you sell digital products directly from Flodesk - useful for creators
  • Flodesk does not have advanced automation branching - if you need complex logic, consider a tool with a more powerful automation engine

The bottom line

Flodesk is one of the most visually polished email tools available. Setup is fast and the templates are genuinely beautiful. If you value aesthetics and want flat-rate pricing, it is worth the setup time.
