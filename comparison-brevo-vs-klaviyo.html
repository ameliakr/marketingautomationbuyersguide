Brevo vs Klaviyo 2026: Affordable Multi-Channel vs Ecommerce Powerhouse

BrevoKlaviyoSMBEcommerceUpdated March 2026

Brevo and Klaviyo serve different markets and the comparison mostly comes down to business type. Brevo is a multi-channel messaging platform with a generous pricing model. Klaviyo is a premium ecommerce email platform that has become the default choice for Shopify stores. If you run an online store, this comparison matters.

Ecommerce features

Klaviyo is built for ecommerce. Deep Shopify and WooCommerce integration, per-campaign revenue tracking, predictive analytics, and best-in-class abandoned cart flows are all Klaviyo strengths. Brevo has ecommerce integrations but ecommerce is not its core focus.

Pricing

Brevo is cheaper, especially at scale. Its send-based pricing means you can have a large list without paying for it. Klaviyo charges by contacts and can become expensive quickly for stores with large subscriber bases. For ecommerce businesses that can monetize their list, Klaviyo's higher cost is often justified by revenue.

Multi-channel

Brevo includes SMS, WhatsApp, and transactional email. Klaviyo added SMS but it is secondary to email. If you want a true multi-channel platform, Brevo is more comprehensive.

Non-ecommerce use

For businesses that do not sell products online, Brevo is the more sensible choice. Klaviyo's pricing and feature set is calibrated for ecommerce: using it for a service business or newsletter means paying for features you will not use.

Choose Brevo if...

  • You do not run an ecommerce store
  • You have a large list and want more affordable pricing
  • You want SMS, email, and transactional email in one platform
  • You want a free plan with unlimited contacts

The bottom line

Klaviyo is the best choice for ecommerce. Brevo is the better choice for everything else, and also a reasonable budget alternative for ecommerce businesses that find Klaviyo too expensive.
