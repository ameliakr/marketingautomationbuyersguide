How to Set Up Iterable: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Iterable Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Iterable is an enterprise-grade cross-channel marketing platform. It is powerful and flexible, but setup requires technical resources - plan for involvement from your engineering team, particularly for API integration and event data setup.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Complete account provisioning
1-2 days

Iterable accounts are provisioned through the sales process. Work with your account team to configure your account settings, user permissions, and initial project setup.

Step 2
Set up your data model
1-3 days

Define your user fields and event schema before importing any data. This is the most important step - a well-designed data model makes everything else easier. Work with your engineering team on this.

Step 3
Configure sending infrastructure
1-2 days

Set up your sending domains with DKIM authentication. Configure dedicated IPs if applicable and follow the IP warming schedule.

Step 4
Integrate your data sources
2-5 days

Connect Iterable to your data sources via API or native integrations (Segment, mParticle, etc.). Work with your engineering team to instrument events correctly.

Step 5
Import your user base
1-2 days

Upload your existing users via CSV or API. Map your user fields to the schema you defined earlier.

Step 6
Build your first journey
1-2 days

Go to Journeys and build your first workflow. Iterable's workflow builder is powerful - start with a welcome series and expand from there.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
30-60 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create your first email campaign. Select your audience using Iterable's segmentation tools, design your email, and send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Iterable

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Iterable setup requires engineering involvement - plan for it from the start
  • Your data model is the foundation of everything - spend time on it before building any campaigns
  • Use Iterable's Catalog feature for personalization at scale - it is one of the platform's strongest capabilities
  • The customer success team is knowledgeable - use them during onboarding
  • Start with simpler journeys and build complexity once the team is comfortable with the platform

The bottom line

Iterable is built for teams with technical resources. The platform is highly capable but the setup complexity reflects that. Work with your Iterable customer success manager and involve your engineering team from day one.
