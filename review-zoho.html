Zoho Campaigns Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Free Plan

Zoho Campaigns Review Updated March 2026

Zoho Campaigns is the email marketing product within Zoho's broader suite of business applications. It is priced more affordably than almost any competitor, includes a free plan for up to 2,000 contacts, and integrates natively with Zoho CRM, Zoho Analytics, and the rest of the Zoho ecosystem. For businesses already using Zoho products, it is a natural and cost-effective choice.

For businesses not in the Zoho ecosystem, the calculus is different. Zoho Campaigns is capable but not exceptional at any single thing. MailerLite is easier to use. Moosend has stronger automation. The main argument for Zoho Campaigns outside the Zoho ecosystem is price - it is genuinely very affordable - but that alone is rarely sufficient reason to choose a tool.

The free plan: the most generous by contact count

Zoho Campaigns' free plan covers up to 2,000 contacts and 6,000 email sends per month. This is more generous than MailerLite's 1,000-contact free plan and significantly more generous than Mailchimp's 500-contact limit. For a small business with a growing list that is not yet ready to pay for email marketing, Zoho Campaigns' free plan is worth considering seriously.

The free plan does include Zoho branding on emails and limits some advanced features, but the contact limit is the most generous available without Kit's creator-specific restrictions.

Pricing: very affordable at every tier

Beyond the free plan, Zoho Campaigns is priced at $3/mo for up to 500 contacts on the Standard plan, scaling with contact count. At 5,000 contacts you are paying around $9/mo. At 10,000 contacts, around $18/mo. These are among the lowest prices in the industry and make Zoho Campaigns a compelling option purely on cost if your needs are straightforward.

Zoho CRM integration: the main differentiator

If you use Zoho CRM, Zoho Campaigns integrates with it natively and bidirectionally. Contact data, lead status, deal stage, and engagement history sync in real time between the two products. Marketing activities in Campaigns show up on contact records in CRM. This level of integration requires Zapier or custom development with most other email marketing tools.

For a small business that runs its sales pipeline in Zoho CRM and wants marketing automation that talks to it cleanly, Zoho Campaigns is the most efficient path. The operational simplicity of keeping everything within the Zoho ecosystem has real value.

Automation

Zoho Campaigns includes a workflow builder that supports standard automation use cases: welcome sequences, drip campaigns, post-form follow-ups, and date-based triggers. The builder is functional but not as visually polished or feature-rich as Moosend or ActiveCampaign. For standard sequences, it covers what most small businesses need. For complex multi-path automations, it will feel limiting.

Ease of use

Zoho's interface has the complexity that comes with being part of a large software suite. It is not as clean or beginner-friendly as MailerLite or Moosend. New users typically need more time to orient than with purpose-built email marketing tools. The help documentation is thorough and support is available, but the onboarding experience is less guided than competitors.

What Zoho Campaigns does not do well

No SMS. The interface is not as polished as dedicated email marketing tools. Automation depth is limited compared to Moosend or ActiveCampaign. The template library is smaller and less current-looking than Mailchimp or Campaign Monitor. And the value proposition outside the Zoho ecosystem is not as compelling as standalone tools at similar prices.

Zoho Campaigns is right for you if...

  • You already use Zoho CRM and want native, bidirectional integration
  • You want the most generous free plan by contact count (2,000 contacts)
  • You are cost-sensitive and want very affordable paid plans at every tier
  • You are building out the Zoho ecosystem and want consolidated billing and support
  • Your automation needs are standard and you do not need complex branching logic

Zoho Campaigns is not right for you if...

  • You are not using Zoho CRM - the primary differentiator disappears
  • You want the most beginner-friendly interface - MailerLite is significantly cleaner
  • You need advanced automation depth - Moosend or ActiveCampaign go further
  • You need SMS marketing alongside email
  • You want the most polished email design tools - competitors do this better

The bottom line

Zoho Campaigns is the right choice for businesses already in the Zoho ecosystem. The CRM integration is the strongest available, the pricing is excellent, and the free plan is the most generous by contact count. For businesses not using Zoho CRM, MailerLite or Moosend are better standalone options at similar prices.
