AWeber Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Better Alternatives

AWeber Review Updated March 2026

AWeber has been around since 1998 and was one of the first email marketing platforms. For a long time it was the go-to tool for bloggers and small businesses. Today it is a reliable, functional platform that has simply been outpaced by newer tools that offer more for less.

AWeber still works. Its deliverability is solid, the interface is familiar, and it gets the job done for basic email marketing. The problem is that MailerLite covers the same ground at a lower price with a better interface, and Moosend offers more automation for the same money. AWeber's main advantage today is familiarity for people who have been using it for years.

What AWeber does well

Deliverability is AWeber's strongest suit. It has built a strong sender reputation over nearly three decades and inbox placement rates are consistently good. The platform is reliable and the infrastructure is solid. For a business where email deliverability is a primary concern, AWeber's track record is real.

The interface is straightforward and experienced users will find it familiar. Customer support is responsive and available by phone as well as chat, which is rare in this space. If you are a long-time AWeber user and the migration cost feels high, there is no pressing reason to leave if it is working for you.

Where AWeber falls short

The automation builder is limited compared to what newer tools offer at the same price. The template library looks dated. Pricing is not competitive - MailerLite offers a better free plan, a cleaner interface, and comparable features for less money. Moosend offers stronger automation at the same starting price. For anyone starting fresh in 2026, it is hard to recommend AWeber over its direct competitors.

AWeber is right for you if...

  • You have been using AWeber for years and the migration cost outweighs the savings
  • Deliverability history and sender reputation matter more than features
  • You want phone support alongside chat and email
  • Your needs are basic and the platform covers everything you use

AWeber is not right for you if...

  • You are starting fresh - MailerLite and Moosend offer more for less
  • You need advanced automation - the builder is limited
  • You are cost-sensitive - better value is available elsewhere
  • You want a modern, polished interface

The bottom line

AWeber is a reliable platform with a long track record, but it has not kept pace with the market. If you are already using it and everything works, staying is a defensible choice.
