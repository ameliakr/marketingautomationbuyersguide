How to Set Up Adobe Marketo: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Adobe Marketo Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Adobe Marketo Engage is a leading B2B marketing automation platform. It is powerful for lead management, ABM, and complex nurture programs. Implementation requires significant planning and typically involves a certified partner.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Complete provisioning with Adobe
1-2 weeks

Work with your Adobe account team to provision your instance. Define your subscription scope, user licenses, and workspace structure.

Step 2
Configure your instance settings
1-2 days

Set up your branding, email settings, sender information, and compliance settings. Configure your workspaces and partitions if applicable.

Step 3
Set up email deliverability
1-3 days

Configure your dedicated sending IPs with DKIM and SPF. Set up your branded tracking links. Follow Marketo's IP warming schedule before sending at volume.

Step 4
Design your database structure
2-3 days

Create your custom fields on Person and Company objects. Define your lead scoring model. Plan your segmentation strategy before importing data.

Step 5
Import your database
1-2 days

Import contacts via CSV or Salesforce sync. Map fields carefully and validate data quality after import.

Step 6
Connect your CRM
1-3 days

If using Salesforce or Microsoft Dynamics, configure the native CRM sync. Define your field mappings and sync rules carefully.

Step 7
Build your first programs
3-7 days

Use Marketo Programs to build your first nurture tracks. Start with a default program for your welcome series and build more complex engagement programs once you are comfortable.

Tips for getting the most out of Adobe Marketo

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Marketo has a steep learning curve - invest in training or hire someone with Marketo experience
  • Your database structure and lead scoring model are foundational - get expert input before finalizing
  • The Salesforce integration is Marketo's most powerful feature for B2B - configure it correctly from the start
  • Marketo Nation (the user community) is an excellent resource for troubleshooting and best practices

The bottom line

Marketo setup is a substantial project. Work with a certified LaunchPoint partner if you do not have in-house Marketo expertise. The platform is highly capable for B2B but the complexity is real - plan accordingly.
