Omnisend vs Brevo 2026: Ecommerce Specialist vs Multi-Channel Generalist

OmnisendBrevoEcommerceSMBUpdated March 2026

Omnisend and Brevo both offer email and SMS marketing, but they target different businesses. Omnisend is built specifically for ecommerce: its automation flows, integrations, and analytics are calibrated for online stores. Brevo is a broader multi-channel messaging platform suited to any type of business.

Ecommerce

Omnisend is the ecommerce-first choice. Deep Shopify and WooCommerce integration, pre-built ecommerce automation flows, and product-level analytics are core to Omnisend. Brevo has ecommerce integrations but ecommerce is not its primary focus.

Multi-channel

Both offer SMS. Brevo also includes WhatsApp and transactional email. Omnisend adds push notifications and web push, which are valuable for ecommerce stores. For non-ecommerce businesses, Brevo's broader channel mix is more useful.

Pricing

Brevo's free plan covers unlimited contacts with 9,000 sends/mo. Omnisend's free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 emails/mo: limited but useful for testing. Brevo is generally cheaper for non-ecommerce use. Omnisend's pricing is justified by ecommerce revenue it generates.

Automation

Both have solid automation. Omnisend's flows are pre-built for ecommerce events: abandoned cart, post-purchase, browse abandonment. Brevo's automation is more general purpose. For online stores, Omnisend's out-of-the-box flows save considerable setup time.

Choose Omnisend if...

  • You run a Shopify or WooCommerce store
  • You want pre-built ecommerce automation flows ready to activate
  • You want push notifications alongside email and SMS
  • Ecommerce-specific analytics and revenue attribution matter to you

The bottom line

Omnisend for ecommerce stores. Brevo for everyone else. Using Omnisend for a non-ecommerce business means paying for features you will not use. Using Brevo for a serious ecommerce store means missing out on specialized tools that drive real revenue.
