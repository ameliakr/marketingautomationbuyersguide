Brevo vs MailerLite 2026: Two Affordable All-Rounders Compared

BrevoMailerLiteComparisonUpdated March 2026

Brevo and MailerLite are two of the most popular affordable email tools, and they are genuinely close in capability. Both offer free plans, solid automation builders, and landing pages. The main differences are in pricing model, SMS, and transactional email.

Brevo charges by email volume rather than by contact count, which makes it unusually cheap if you have a large list but do not email everyone often. MailerLite charges by subscriber and is simpler overall.

Pricing model difference

This is the most important difference between the two tools. Brevo prices by the number of emails you send, not by how many contacts you have. If you have 50,000 contacts but only email 5,000 of them each month, Brevo can be significantly cheaper than MailerLite.

MailerLite prices by subscriber count. If you email your full list every week, MailerLite may end up similarly priced. The right choice depends on your send frequency relative to list size.

SMS and transactional email

Brevo includes SMS marketing and transactional email (order confirmations, password resets) in the same platform. This makes it a broader tool for businesses that need both marketing and transactional messaging.

MailerLite does not have SMS or transactional email. If you need either, Brevo is the better fit.

Ease of use

MailerLite has a slight edge on simplicity. The interface is cleaner and easier for beginners to navigate. Brevo is also accessible but has more features, which adds some complexity.

Automation

Both have capable visual automation builders. MailerLite handles standard sequences well. Brevo adds more advanced segmentation options and multi-channel workflows including SMS alongside email.

Choose Brevo if...

  • You have a large list but only email a portion of it regularly
  • You need SMS marketing alongside email
  • You send transactional emails and want them in the same platform
  • Multi-channel campaigns are part of your strategy
