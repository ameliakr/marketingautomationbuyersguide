How to Set Up Campaign Monitor: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Campaign Monitor Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Campaign Monitor is known for its email design quality. The template library is best-in-class and the email builder is precise and polished. Setup is straightforward, though it is more expensive than most alternatives at equivalent tiers.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your account
5-10 minutes

Go to campaignmonitor.com and sign up. You can trial the platform before purchasing a plan. Enter your company details and confirm your account.

Step 2
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Account Settings, then Verified Sending Domains. Add your domain and follow the instructions to add DKIM records to your DNS. Verify once the records have propagated.

Step 3
Create a list
5-10 minutes

Go to Lists and Subscribers and create your first list. Give it a name and configure your unsubscribe settings and confirmation email.

Step 4
Import your subscribers
10-20 minutes

Upload your CSV file to your list. Map columns to Campaign Monitor fields. Choose whether to send a confirmation email to imported subscribers.

Step 5
Build your first template
30-60 minutes

Go to Email Templates and either choose from the library or build a custom template. Campaign Monitor's drag-and-drop builder is precise - spend time getting your template right as you will reuse it.

Step 6
Set up a welcome automation
20-30 minutes

Go to Automation and create a new journey. Set the entry trigger to list subscription. Add an email action and use or customize your template for the welcome message. Activate the journey.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a new campaign. Select your list, choose your template, write your content, preview and test, then send or schedule.

Tips for getting the most out of Campaign Monitor

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Campaign Monitor's template builder is its strongest feature - invest time in building a polished reusable template
  • Use the Inbox Preview tool to check how your emails render across different email clients before sending
  • Campaign Monitor's segmentation is powerful - use custom fields to enable precise targeting
  • The Transactional Email add-on (Mailgun) is available if you need to send triggered transactional emails alongside marketing campaigns

The bottom line

Campaign Monitor is the right tool when email design quality matters. Set up your domain authentication first, then focus on building templates that reflect your brand well. The platform rewards the time you put into design.
