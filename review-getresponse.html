GetResponse Review 2026: Pricing, Features, and Automation

GetResponse Review Updated March 2026

GetResponse positions itself as an all-in-one marketing platform, and unusually for a tool that makes that claim, it largely delivers on it. Email marketing, automation, landing pages, webinars, SMS, and paid ad management are all included at various pricing tiers. If you want to reduce the number of subscriptions you are managing, GetResponse is worth a serious look.

The tradeoff is that being a generalist means it is not the best at any single thing. The automation builder is good but not as deep as ActiveCampaign's. The landing pages are solid but not as polished as dedicated tools. And the interface, while functional, is not the most intuitive in the market. This review helps you decide whether the breadth is worth those tradeoffs.

Email and automation

GetResponse's automation builder uses a visual canvas interface that supports multiple triggers, conditions, and branching logic. It is more capable than Mailchimp's Customer Journeys and covers the standard use cases well: welcome sequences, abandoned cart emails, post-purchase flows, and lead nurture. It does not match ActiveCampaign's depth, but for most small to mid-size businesses it is more than sufficient.

The email editor is solid, with a good template library and a drag-and-drop builder that produces clean emails across clients. A/B testing is available on paid plans.

Webinars: the standout feature

GetResponse's webinar functionality is the feature that sets it apart from most competitors. You can host webinars directly from the platform, with attendee registration, automated email reminders, recording, and follow-up sequences all handled in one place. For a business that runs regular online events, this eliminates the need for a separate webinar tool like Zoom Webinars or Demio.

The webinar tool is not as feature-rich as dedicated platforms, but for straightforward presentations and Q&A sessions it works well and the integration with the rest of the platform is seamless.

Landing pages

GetResponse includes a landing page builder on all plans including free. The builder is functional with a reasonable template selection. Pages are mobile-responsive and can use a custom domain on paid plans. For basic lead capture pages and webinar registration pages, it does everything you need without requiring a separate tool.

Pricing

GetResponse's pricing structure is more complex than most. The free plan covers 500 contacts. Email Marketing starts at $15/mo, Marketing Automation at $49/mo, and E-commerce Marketing at $99/mo. Webinars are included from the Marketing Automation tier upward. The value calculation depends heavily on which features you actually use - if you need webinars and automation, the $49/mo plan replaces two or three separate tools.

Ease of use

GetResponse's interface is functional but not as clean as MailerLite or as polished as HubSpot. New users typically need a day or two to get fully oriented. The navigation can feel cluttered given the number of features, and some settings are not where you would expect to find them. The help documentation is thorough and live chat support is available on paid plans.

GetResponse is right for you if...

  • You want email, webinars, and landing pages without managing three separate subscriptions
  • You run regular online events and want registration and follow-up handled automatically
  • You need SMS alongside email without paying for an additional tool
  • You want a visual automation builder with more capability than Mailchimp offers
  • You are price-sensitive and the all-in-one value calculation works in your favor

GetResponse is not right for you if...

  • You need deep CRM functionality - GetResponse does not have a built-in CRM
  • You want the cleanest, most intuitive interface available - MailerLite is easier
  • You need enterprise-grade automation depth - ActiveCampaign goes further
  • You run a large e-commerce store - Klaviyo or Omnisend are better specialized fits
  • You just need simple email campaigns - the feature set is more than you need

The bottom line

GetResponse is the right choice when the combination of email, automation, webinars, and landing pages in one platform makes financial sense for your business. It is not the best at any single one of those things, but if you need several of them, the consolidation value is real.
