GetResponse vs ActiveCampaign 2026: All-in-One Platform vs Deep Automation

GetResponseActiveCampaignComparisonUpdated March 2026

GetResponse and ActiveCampaign are both serious marketing automation platforms, but they prioritize different things. GetResponse is a genuine all-in-one tool: email, automation, webinars, landing pages, and paid ads under one roof. ActiveCampaign goes deeper on automation and CRM, with a contact management system that no comparable tool matches.

If your business runs webinars or wants a single platform for email plus paid acquisition, GetResponse is worth a close look. If you need sophisticated multi-step automation and a real CRM pipeline, ActiveCampaign has the edge.

Automation depth

ActiveCampaign has the more powerful automation builder. You get 75+ triggers, unlimited actions per workflow, goal-based logic, and automations that can trigger other automations. It is designed for complex multi-branch journeys.

GetResponse has a solid visual automation builder that handles standard use cases well. Welcome series, post-purchase flows, lead nurture sequences. It handles most businesses' automation needs, but you will notice limits if your workflows become complex.

Webinars and all-in-one features

GetResponse is one of the few email platforms with a native webinar tool. You can host webinars, manage registrations, and trigger automations based on attendance. For businesses that run webinars regularly, this removes the need for a separate tool like Zoom Webinars or Demio.

ActiveCampaign does not have a webinar tool. If webinars are central to your marketing, GetResponse wins this comparison.

CRM and sales pipeline

ActiveCampaign includes a full built-in CRM with deal pipelines, lead scoring, and sales automation. Marketing and sales data share the same contact record. GetResponse has basic contact management but no real pipeline or scoring system.

For B2B businesses or service companies managing sales alongside marketing, ActiveCampaign is the stronger choice.

Pricing

GetResponse has a free plan covering 500 contacts. Paid plans start at $19/mo. ActiveCampaign starts at $15/mo with no free plan, but the 14-day trial gives full platform access.

At comparable contact counts, pricing is similar. GetResponse becomes better value if you use the webinar feature, since that would otherwise require a separate tool.

The bottom line

GetResponse is the better choice if you run webinars or want a broader all-in-one platform. ActiveCampaign is the better choice if deep automation and CRM are what your business actually needs. Both are strong tools. The decision comes down to which capability matters more to you.
