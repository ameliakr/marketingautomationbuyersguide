GetResponse and ActiveCampaign are both capable platforms that go well beyond basic email marketing. Both include automation builders, landing pages, SMS, and e-commerce integrations. The webinar advantage

GetResponse is unique in including webinar hosting at paid tiers. If you run online events, workshops, or courses as part of your marketing, this is a meaningful feature that saves you from paying for a separate tool. No other major email platform includes this at comparable pricing.

CRM and automation

ActiveCampaign has the deeper CRM and more advanced automation builder. Deal pipelines, contact scoring, and complex branching workflows are more developed in ActiveCampaign. If your business runs on a sales pipeline and needs email marketing connected to it, ActiveCampaign is the stronger choice.

Pricing

Both start at $15/mo. GetResponse has a free plan; ActiveCampaign does not. GetResponse's paid plans tend to be slightly cheaper at comparable feature levels. ActiveCampaign justifies the premium with more automation depth and a stronger CRM.

Choose GetResponse if...

  • You run webinars or online events and want them integrated with your email marketing
  • You want a free plan to start without a credit card
  • You need landing pages, SMS, and automation in one tool at a competitive price
  • Your automation needs are solid but not extremely complex
