How to Set Up Mailchimp: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Mailchimp Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Mailchimp is the most widely used email marketing platform and has the most community documentation, tutorials, and third-party resources of any tool on this list. Getting started is well-supported. This guide covers the essentials to get you from signup to first send.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Mailchimp account
5-10 minutes

Go to mailchimp.com and sign up for free. The free plan covers up to 500 contacts and 1,000 sends per month. Enter your name, business name, and website. Mailchimp will walk you through a brief onboarding flow.

Step 2
Complete your profile and business details
10 minutes

Mailchimp requires a physical mailing address in every email you send - this is a legal requirement under CAN-SPAM. Add your address in your profile settings. Also set your default From name and From email address. Use an email on your own domain rather than a Gmail or Yahoo address for better deliverability.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Account, then Settings, then Domains and authenticate your sending domain. Mailchimp provides DKIM records to add to your DNS. On paid plans you can also set up custom domain authentication for better deliverability. This step is optional on the free plan but recommended.

Step 4
Set up your audience
10-15 minutes

Mailchimp organizes contacts into Audiences. Your free account includes one audience. Go to Audience, then Audience Dashboard, and configure your audience settings. Create any tags or groups you need to segment your list.

Step 5
Create a signup form
15-20 minutes

Go to Audience, then Signup Forms. Mailchimp offers several form types - embedded, popup, and a hosted landing page. Customize the fields and connect it to your audience. Mailchimp also integrates natively with WordPress, Shopify, and many other platforms.

Step 6
Build a welcome email
20-30 minutes

Go to Automations and create a Welcome new subscribers journey. This sends automatically when someone joins your audience. Write a warm, personal welcome email and activate the automation. This is the highest-impact thing you can set up on day one.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a Regular email. Select your audience, write your subject line and preview text, and design your email using the drag-and-drop editor. Use the Preview mode and send yourself a test before going live.

Tips for getting the most out of Mailchimp

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Mailchimp's free plan limits you to 500 contacts and 1,000 sends per month - plan ahead for when you will hit these limits
  • Use merge tags to personalize your emails with subscriber names - Mailchimp supports these in subject lines and email body
  • The Content Studio lets you store and reuse brand assets - upload your logo and brand colors on day one
  • Mailchimp's audience management is less flexible than tools like ActiveCampaign or Moosend - plan your tagging and group structure before importing contacts
  • Check your email's spam score in the campaign builder before sending - Mailchimp flags common spam triggers

The bottom line

Mailchimp is a reliable starting point for email marketing. Get your audience set up, your domain authenticated, and your welcome automation running before you focus on campaigns.
