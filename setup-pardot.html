How to Set Up Pardot (Salesforce): A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Pardot (Salesforce) Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Pardot (now called Marketing Cloud Account Engagement) is Salesforce's B2B marketing automation product. It is tightly integrated with Salesforce CRM, making it the natural choice for B2B companies already on the Salesforce platform.

Contact Salesforce to get started with Pardot.
→ Contact Salesforce sales

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Configure Pardot with Salesforce
1-3 days

Pardot is provisioned through Salesforce. Work with your Salesforce admin to connect your Pardot account and configure the Pardot-Salesforce connector. Define your syncing rules and field mappings.

Step 2
Set up email authentication
15-30 minutes

Configure your sending domains in Pardot with DKIM authentication. Add tracker domains for link tracking.

Step 3
Configure your Pardot settings
1-2 hours

Set up your company profile, email settings, visitor filters (to exclude internal traffic from tracking), and compliance settings.

Step 4
Import your prospects
1-2 hours

Import contacts as Pardot Prospects via CSV. Map fields to Pardot's standard and custom fields. Prospects will sync to Salesforce based on your connector rules.

Step 5
Build your forms and landing pages
1-2 hours

Create Pardot forms for your key conversion points. Build or connect landing pages. Forms are central to Pardot's tracking and scoring model.

Step 6
Set up lead scoring and grading
1-2 hours

Configure your scoring rules (behavioral) and grading model (profile fit). Align these with your sales team's definition of a sales-qualified lead.

Step 7
Build your first engagement program
2-4 hours

Go to Automation, then Engagement Studio. Build your first nurture program. Engagement Studio is Pardot's campaign builder - it is visual and relatively straightforward once you understand the logic.

Tips for getting the most out of Pardot (Salesforce)

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Pardot and Salesforce are deeply connected - make sure your Salesforce admin is involved in setup
  • Forms are the foundation of Pardot's tracking - build them in Pardot rather than using non-Pardot forms where possible
  • Lead scoring and grading require alignment with your sales team - get their input before configuring
  • Pardot's Engagement Studio is significantly better than older list-based email tools - use it for all nurture programs

The bottom line

Pardot is most powerful when used alongside Salesforce CRM. If you are already a Salesforce shop, the integration makes it the obvious choice. Setup complexity depends heavily on your Salesforce configuration - work with a certified Pardot consultant.
