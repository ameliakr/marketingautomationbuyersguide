MailerLite vs Moosend 2026: which is better value?

MailerLite Moosend SMB Automation Updated March 2026

MailerLite and Moosend are two of the best-value email marketing tools available. Both sit at the affordable end of the market, both have solid automation, and both are genuinely capable of handling the needs of a small to mid-size business. The differences are real but subtle -- this is a comparison of two good options, not a clear winner versus a runner-up.

The main differences come down to the free plan, the depth of automation, and design capabilities. MailerLite has a generous free plan and a more polished email editor. Moosend has slightly deeper automation at comparable prices and no free plan but a 30-day trial.

Pricing

Both tools start at $9/mo and scale by contact count, so pricing is closely matched across tiers. The biggest practical difference is the free plan. MailerLite offers a free plan up to 1,000 subscribers that includes automation, landing pages, and most core features. This makes it the clear choice if you want to start without a paid commitment. Moosend's 30-day trial gives you more time to fully evaluate before deciding, but you do need to upgrade to continue.

At larger list sizes, prices remain competitive. Neither tool has a significant cost advantage over the other -- the decision should be made on features and fit, not price.

Automation

This is where Moosend has a genuine edge. Its automation builder supports more complex conditional logic, a wider range of trigger types, and some distinctive features like weather-based triggers that MailerLite does not offer. For a business that wants to build sophisticated multi-step sequences with branching paths and behavioral conditions, Moosend goes further.

MailerLite's automation is not weak -- it covers the standard use cases well and is easier to learn. For most small businesses building welcome sequences, re-engagement flows, and basic nurture tracks, MailerLite is more than sufficient. The gap only becomes meaningful when you need the more advanced conditional logic Moosend supports.

Email editor and design

MailerLite's drag-and-drop editor is better. The interface is cleaner, the template library is more polished, and the overall design output is more refined. If how your emails look matters to your brand -- retail, creative businesses, agencies -- MailerLite is the stronger choice here.

Moosend's editor is capable and easy to use, but it is not the standout feature. Templates are functional rather than striking. For businesses where email design is not a priority, the difference is minor.

Ease of use

Both tools are genuinely easy to learn. MailerLite is slightly more beginner-friendly -- the interface is clean, onboarding is guided, and the learning curve is shallow. Moosend is also accessible but the automation builder requires slightly more familiarity before you get the most out of it.

Neither is a tool that will frustrate a non-technical user. Either is a reasonable first email platform.

Integrations and ecosystem

MailerLite connects with Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, and a wide range of third-party tools via Zapier and native integrations. It also includes a website builder and blog on paid plans, which makes it a more complete platform for some users. Moosend has a solid integration set and connects with the major e-commerce platforms, but the broader platform ecosystem is smaller than MailerLite's.

The bottom line

If you are starting out and want to grow into a platform without paying first, MailerLite is the easier choice -- the free plan is generous, the editor is excellent, and it covers most use cases well. If automation is the core of what you are building and you want the most capability for the price, Moosend goes further.

Either is a strong choice at this price point. The decision mostly comes down to whether you need a free plan and great design (MailerLite) or maximum automation depth (Moosend).
