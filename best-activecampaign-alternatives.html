Best ActiveCampaign Alternatives 2026

ActiveCampaignAlternativesRoundupUpdated March 2026

ActiveCampaign is one of the best marketing automation platforms at the SMB level, but it is not always the right fit. If the price is too high, the interface is more complex than you need, or you want ecommerce-specific features, these alternatives are worth evaluating.

Why look for an ActiveCampaign alternative

ActiveCampaign is one of the best automation platforms at the SMB level, but it is not for everyone. The most common reasons to look elsewhere: the price is too high (especially at larger contact counts), the interface is complex for simple use cases, or you need ecommerce-specific tools that ActiveCampaign does not prioritize.

Brevo Best budget alternative

Brevo is the most commonly considered alternative for businesses that want good automation at a lower price. It doesn't match ActiveCampaign's depth but covers most standard automation use cases for significantly less money — especially if you have a large list.

  • Significantly cheaper, especially for large lists
  • SMS and transactional email included
  • Unlimited contacts on free plan
  • Solid automation for standard workflows

Moosend Best value automation alternative

Moosend's automation is closer to ActiveCampaign's than most tools in its price range. Complex workflows, conditional logic, and behavioral triggers are all available. If you need automation capability without ActiveCampaign's price, Moosend is the best comparison.

  • Best automation per dollar outside of ActiveCampaign
  • Significantly cheaper at most list sizes
  • 30-day free trial
  • Worth a serious evaluation before committing to AC

MailerLite Best for simpler needs

If you were using ActiveCampaign but only needed basic automation sequences, MailerLite is a simpler and cheaper option. It won't match AC's depth but covers welcome sequences, nurture emails, and basic conditional logic at a much lower price.

  • Much cheaper than ActiveCampaign
  • Easier to use
  • Good free plan
  • Right if you were not using AC to its full potential
→ Try MailerLite free
Klaviyo Best for ecommerce

If the reason you are looking away from ActiveCampaign is that you run an ecommerce store and want more revenue-specific features, Klaviyo is the move. It is more expensive but purpose-built for ecommerce automation in a way AC is not.

  • Best ecommerce automation available
  • Revenue attribution and predictive analytics
  • Deep Shopify integration
  • Worth the premium if ecommerce drives your revenue
→ Try Klaviyo free

What to consider before switching

ActiveCampaign's automation is genuinely excellent. Before switching, make sure you have actually hit its limits or that the price is genuinely unjustifiable. If you are using AC well, you are getting real value from it. Migrating automation workflows is more work than migrating contacts — factor that into the decision.
