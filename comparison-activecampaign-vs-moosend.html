ActiveCampaign vs Moosend 2026: Two Strong Automation Tools Compared

ActiveCampaignMoosendSMBAutomationUpdated March 2026

ActiveCampaign and Moosend are both serious automation tools aimed at small and mid-size businesses. Both have visual automation builders, e-commerce integrations, and pricing that scales by contact count. The gap between them is not about basic functionality - both handle that well. It is about depth.

ActiveCampaign goes further on CRM, SMS, and automation complexity. Moosend is more affordable and more than capable for businesses that do not need those extras.

Pricing

Moosend starts at $9/mo vs ActiveCampaign's $15/mo, and that gap widens as your list grows. Moosend also offers a longer 30-day trial compared to ActiveCampaign's 14 days. If price is a deciding factor, Moosend is the more affordable choice at every tier.

Automation

Both tools have capable visual automation builders. ActiveCampaign has more advanced features including predictive sending, win probability scoring, and a wider range of triggers. Moosend's builder is excellent for standard automation needs and handles multi-step sequences reliably.

For most small businesses, both tools offer more automation capability than they will ever fully use. The difference only matters if you are building genuinely complex multi-branch workflows.

CRM and sales

This is the clearest difference. ActiveCampaign has a built-in CRM with deal pipelines, contact scoring, and task assignment. Moosend has no CRM. If you manage a sales pipeline alongside your email marketing, ActiveCampaign is the only choice between these two.

E-commerce

Both tools integrate with Shopify and WooCommerce and support abandoned cart, browse abandonment, and product recommendation automations. Moosend has strong e-commerce features for its price point. ActiveCampaign goes further with deeper segmentation options and more integration partners overall.

Choose ActiveCampaign if...

  • You need a built-in CRM to manage leads and deals
  • You want SMS marketing alongside email automation
  • Your automation workflows are complex with multiple branches
  • You are a B2B company or service business managing a sales pipeline
