AWeber vs Moosend 2026: Reliable Classic vs Automation Value Leader

AWeberMoosendComparisonUpdated March 2026

AWeber and Moosend are both affordable email marketing tools aimed at small businesses, but they come from different eras. AWeber is one of the oldest email platforms and trades on reliability, strong support, and simplicity. Moosend is a newer entrant that packs more automation power into a lower price point.

For most businesses comparing these two, Moosend offers more for less. AWeber remains the better choice if you specifically want a free plan or value AWeber's long-standing customer support reputation.

Automation

Moosend has a more capable automation builder than AWeber at this price point. You get a visual workflow editor with good conditional logic, multi-step sequences, and strong behavioral triggers. For the price, it punches above its weight.

AWeber's automation is functional but simpler. It covers the basics well: welcome sequences, tagging-based workflows, and basic drip campaigns.

Pricing

Moosend is cheaper on paid plans, starting at $9/mo versus AWeber's $15/mo. AWeber's advantage is a free plan for up to 500 subscribers, which Moosend does not offer (just a 30-day trial).

If you need a free plan, AWeber wins. If you are ready to pay, Moosend offers more for less.

E-commerce capabilities

Moosend has stronger e-commerce features, including product recommendation blocks in emails, cart abandonment flows, and purchase-based segmentation. AWeber's e-commerce integrations are more basic.

For online stores, Moosend is the better fit between these two.

Support

AWeber offers phone and live chat support on paid plans. This is genuinely rare for tools at this price and is often cited as a reason people stick with AWeber despite competing tools offering more features.
