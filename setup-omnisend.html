How to Set Up Omnisend: A Step-by-Step Getting Started Guide

Omnisend Setup Guide Updated March 2026

Omnisend is built for e-commerce stores and most of the setup is oriented around your store connection. Once you have connected Shopify or WooCommerce, a significant amount of data and automation infrastructure is available immediately. This guide walks you through the full setup for an online store.

Step-by-step setup guide

Step 1
Create your Omnisend account
5 minutes

Go to omnisend.com and sign up. The free plan covers 250 contacts and 500 emails per month plus 60 SMS messages. Enter your business details and connect your store in the next step.

Step 2
Connect your e-commerce store
10-20 minutes

This is the most important setup step. Go to Store Settings and connect your Shopify or WooCommerce store. Omnisend will import your products, past orders, and customer data automatically. This connection powers your abandoned cart automation, product recommendation emails, and purchase-triggered flows.

Step 3
Authenticate your sending domain
15-30 minutes

Go to Store Settings, then Email, then Sender Domain. Add your domain and follow the DKIM authentication instructions. Authenticated emails have significantly better deliverability than unauthenticated ones.

Step 4
Activate your pre-built automation flows
20-30 minutes

Go to Automation and browse the pre-built workflow templates. Omnisend includes templates for abandoned cart, abandoned browse, welcome series, post-purchase, product review request, and win-back. Start by activating abandoned cart - it typically generates the highest revenue of any e-commerce automation. Customize the email content and activate each flow.

Step 5
Set up your signup form
15-20 minutes

Go to Forms and create a popup or teaser bar. Omnisend's forms can collect email and phone number simultaneously for SMS opt-in. This is more valuable than email-only forms if you plan to use SMS. Configure the form timing and display rules, then add it to your store.

Step 6
Configure SMS (optional)
15-20 minutes

Go to Channels, then SMS, and set up your SMS sending. Omnisend SMS is included in your plan - you get 60 free SMS messages per month on the free plan. Add SMS steps to your automation flows, particularly the abandoned cart sequence. A well-timed SMS after an email in an abandoned cart flow significantly increases recovery rates.

Step 7
Send your first campaign
20-30 minutes

Go to Campaigns and create a new email campaign. Select your subscriber list, design your email using the drag-and-drop editor, and add a product block to pull live product data from your store. Write your subject line and send a test before going live.

Tips for getting the most out of Omnisend

Things worth knowing before you start
  • Activate the abandoned cart automation first - it is the highest-revenue automation for most e-commerce stores
  • Collect phone numbers alongside emails in your signup form from day one - SMS opt-in rates drop when asked later
  • Omnisend's product blocks pull live inventory data from your store - use them in every promotional email
  • Set up the post-purchase flow early - it drives reviews, repeat purchases, and upsells with minimal ongoing effort
  • The free plan's 250 contact limit is low - plan your upgrade timing before you hit it, especially if you are actively growing your list

The bottom line

Omnisend is fast to set up for e-commerce stores because so much is pre-built. Connect your store, activate your abandoned cart flow, and get your signup form live - those three things alone will generate meaningful returns before you even send your first campaign.
