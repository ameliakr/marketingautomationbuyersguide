How to Migrate from ActiveCampaign to HubSpot

ActiveCampaign HubSpot Migration Updated March 2026

Moving from ActiveCampaign to HubSpot usually means your business has grown to the point where CRM is as important as email automation. ActiveCampaign has a capable CRM, but HubSpot's is deeper - more structured deal pipelines, better sales reporting, and tighter integration between marketing and sales activities.

This is not a simple migration. Both platforms are powerful and both organize data differently. The contact migration is standard. The automation rebuild is where the complexity lies - ActiveCampaign's automation engine is different from HubSpot's workflow builder, and translating complex sequences requires careful planning.

Step-by-step migration guide

Step 1
Export your contacts from ActiveCampaign
15-30 minutes

Export your full contact list as a CSV including all custom fields and tags. Export your unsubscribe and bounce lists separately.

Step 2
Document your automations
30-90 minutes

Screenshot or write down every active automation in ActiveCampaign - the trigger, conditions, email content, and timing between steps. Do this before you cancel your account.

Step 3
Export your email templates
30-60 minutes

Save the HTML of any email templates you want to reuse. You can import HTML into HubSpot or rebuild templates using their editor.

Step 4
Download your campaign reports
10-15 minutes

Export your historical performance data. Open rates, click rates, and engagement history will not carry over and you lose access once your account is closed.

Step 5
Set up your HubSpot account
30-60 minutes

Complete account setup including domain authentication. Add SPF and DKIM records to your DNS settings. This is essential for deliverability and HubSpot will guide you through it.

Step 6
Import your contacts
20-45 minutes

Import your CSV into HubSpot and map each column to the correct field. Import your suppression list separately to prevent emailing contacts who have opted out.

Step 7
Rebuild your automations
1-4 hours

Use HubSpot's automation builder to recreate your workflows. Start with the highest-priority sequences and test each one before activating.

Step 8
Update your forms and integrations
30-60 minutes

Replace your ActiveCampaign forms with HubSpot forms on your website. Update any integrations, popups, or third-party tools connected to ActiveCampaign.

Step 9
Test everything before going live
1-2 hours

Send test emails. Trigger each automation with a test contact. Confirm domain authentication is passing. Do not cancel ActiveCampaign until this is done.

Step 10
Cancel ActiveCampaign
5 minutes

Once HubSpot is fully set up and tested, cancel your ActiveCampaign subscription. Check your billing date first to avoid being charged for another month.

Before you cancel ActiveCampaign

Export your contact list, suppression list, campaign history, and templates. Update all connected integrations to point to HubSpot. Once your ActiveCampaign account is closed, this data is gone.

Key differences to plan for

Automation logic works differently. ActiveCampaign's automation builder uses a canvas with multiple start triggers and complex branching. HubSpot's workflow builder is more linear and the trigger and action options differ. Do not expect a one-to-one translation - some ActiveCampaign automations will need to be rethought rather than simply rebuilt.

CRM data needs careful mapping. If you have deal data in ActiveCampaign, plan how that maps to HubSpot deals, companies, and contacts before migrating. HubSpot's data model uses Companies as a separate object that associates with Contacts and Deals. ActiveCampaign's is simpler. Budget extra time for this if your CRM data is extensive.

HubSpot Marketing Hub tiers matter. Basic automation is on Starter. Advanced workflows with branching are on Professional at $800/mo. If you are coming from ActiveCampaign's Plus plan, make sure you understand which HubSpot tier gives you equivalent functionality before committing.

Why people switch from ActiveCampaign to HubSpot

The most common driver is sales team growth. When a business adds salespeople who need pipeline visibility, reporting, and contact management at scale, HubSpot's CRM becomes more compelling than ActiveCampaign's. HubSpot was built CRM-first. ActiveCampaign was built automation-first. When CRM becomes the priority, HubSpot wins.

The bottom line

Migrating from ActiveCampaign to HubSpot is manageable for most businesses. Your contacts move over cleanly. The work is in rebuilding your automations and updating your forms. Keep both accounts running in parallel until everything is confirmed working, then cancel ActiveCampaign.
